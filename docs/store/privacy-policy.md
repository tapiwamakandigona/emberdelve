# Emberdelve — Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 2026-07-25_

Emberdelve ("the app", `com.tsorostudios.emberdelve`) is a single-player
pixel action-platformer by Tsoro Studios (Tapiwa Makandigona).

## The short version

**Emberdelve collects no personal data.** The only thing it can ever send is
**anonymous gameplay statistics, and only if you explicitly opt in** — it is
off by default and can be turned off again any time in Settings.

## Details

- **No personal data collected.** No accounts, no names, no email addresses,
  no contacts, no location, no advertising identifiers, no free-text input.
- **Optional, opt-in gameplay analytics.** On first launch the app asks
  whether you want to share anonymous gameplay statistics (which levels you
  play, whether you finish them, and which settings you use) to help us fix
  bugs and improve the game. **Nothing is sent unless you tap "Allow"**, and
  you can change your choice any time in Settings → "Gameplay analytics".
  If you opt in, this data is processed on our behalf by **Google Firebase
  Analytics** ([privacy notice](https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy)).
  Ad personalization signals are disabled and the app never accesses the
  advertising ID.
- **No ads, no trackers, no data sales.** The app contains no advertising
  SDKs and no third-party tracking beyond the optional analytics described
  above. We never sell or share data.
- **Local save data only.** Your game progress (coins, unlocks, settings)
  is stored only in the app's private storage on your device. Deleting the
  app deletes it. It never leaves your device.
- **Permissions.** `VIBRATE` (optional in-game haptics) and
  `INTERNET`/`ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE` (used solely for the optional analytics
  above; if you never opt in, nothing is transmitted). The Google Analytics
  library also adds `WAKE_LOCK` and a Play Store install-referrer binding of
  its own; the advertising-ID permissions it would normally add are removed
  from our build. No permission requires a runtime prompt.
- **Children.** The app collects no personal data from anyone, including
  children. Analytics, when enabled, are anonymous gameplay statistics only.

## Google Play data-safety summary

| Question | Answer |
| --- | --- |
| Does the app collect or share user data? | Only optional, user-enabled anonymous app-interaction analytics (Firebase Analytics); no personal data; nothing shared with third parties for their own purposes |
| Is data collection optional? | Yes — off by default, opt-in, revocable in Settings |
| Data encrypted in transit? | Yes (when analytics are enabled) |
| Can users request data deletion? | Turning analytics off stops collection; uninstalling removes local saves. For analytics-data deletion requests, contact the developer via the Play listing |

## Changes & contact

If a future version changes any of the above, this policy will be updated
first and the change called out in the release notes.

Questions: contact the developer via the Google Play listing for Emberdelve.
