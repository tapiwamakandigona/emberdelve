Borderline Rich — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-10-03 · com.borderlinerich.game · published by Tsoro Studios (Tapiwa Makandigona)
The short version: Borderline Rich collects no data at all. Nothing about you or your game ever leaves your phone.

Details

  • No personal data collected. No accounts, names, email addresses, contacts, location, photos, advertising identifiers or free-text input.
  • No analytics, no ads, no trackers. The app contains no analytics, advertising or crash-reporting SDKs and makes no network requests. Every asset (art, fonts, sound) is bundled in the app or generated on the device.
  • Local save data only. Your progress and settings are stored in the app’s private storage on your device. Uninstalling the app deletes them.
  • Purchases. Test builds contain a clearly labelled sandbox store: no real money is ever charged and no payment data is handled. If real in-app purchases are added later they will go through Google Play Billing, and this policy will be updated first.
  • Permissions. INTERNET is declared by the app framework (Capacitor) but unused by the game. No permission requires a runtime prompt.
  • Children. The app collects no personal data from anyone, including children.

If a future version changes any of the above, this policy will be updated first and the change called out in the release notes. Questions: contact the developer through the Google Play listing.
