Pyregrove — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-08-31 · com.tsorostudios.pyregrove

Pyregrove (“the app”) is a single-player pixel action-platformer by Tsoro Studios (Tapiwa Makandigona). Everything here applies equally to the earlier alpha builds of the same game published as “Emberwood” / “Emberdelve v2” (com.tsorostudios.emberwood).

The short version: Pyregrove collects no personal data. The only thing it can ever send is anonymous gameplay statistics, and only if you explicitly opt in — off by default, revocable any time in Settings.

Details

  • No personal data collected. No accounts, no names, no email addresses, no contacts, no location, no advertising identifiers, no free-text input.
  • Optional, opt-in gameplay analytics. On first launch the app asks whether you want to share anonymous gameplay statistics (which levels you play, whether you finish them, and which settings you use) to help us fix bugs and improve the game. Nothing is sent unless you tap “Allow”, and you can change your choice any time in Settings → “Gameplay analytics”. If you opt in, this data is processed on our behalf by Google Firebase Analytics. Ad personalization signals are disabled and the app never accesses the advertising ID.
  • No ads, no trackers, no data sales. The app contains no advertising SDKs and no third-party tracking beyond the optional analytics described above. We never sell or share data.
  • Local save data only. Your game progress (coins, unlocks, settings) is stored only in the app’s private storage on your device. Deleting the app deletes it. It never leaves your device.
  • Permissions. VIBRATE (optional in-game haptics) and INTERNET/ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE (used solely for the optional analytics above; if you never opt in, nothing is transmitted). The Google Analytics library also adds WAKE_LOCK and a Play Store install-referrer binding of its own; the advertising-ID permissions it would normally add are removed from our build. No permission requires a runtime prompt.
  • Children. The app collects no personal data from anyone, including children. Analytics, when enabled, are anonymous gameplay statistics only.

Changes & contact

If a future version ever changes any of the above (for example, optional cloud save), this policy will be updated first and the change called out in the release notes.

Questions: contact the developer via GitHub (tapiwamakandigona) or, once the app is on Google Play, via its Play listing.
