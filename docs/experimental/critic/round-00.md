# Emberdelve — Round 0 baseline critique (independent critic, read-only)

Evidence reviewed: evidence pack round-00 — MANIFEST.md, audio_report.md, all 18 contact sheets, full-res frames zoomed for 000/003/004/006/008/016/021/024/040 (play_session) and 01/05 (fresh_walk 320/412); C_* strips cropped/upscaled . Grounding reads (no edits): `lib/game/tour.dart`, `lib/ui/screens/tour_overlay.dart`, `lib/ui/screens/tutorial_overlay.dart`, `lib/ui/screens/combat_screen.dart` (kill hold, `_attack`), `lib/audio/audio_service.dart` (event→SFX map, 21 SFX), `assets/images/sprite_meta.json`, enemy/character PNG dimensions, `tool/fresh_walk_visual_test.dart`, `tool/play_session_test.dart`. No previous backlog ids were supplied, so `closed`/`regressions` are empty; the Sept 13 scores (art 6 / animation 4 / depth 7) are used as the only delta reference.

## Verdict
Emberdelve has a genuinely handsome UI shell (typography, dice, cards, settings) wrapped around a combat stage that still reads as placeholder: 16-px blob foes that never move to hit you, a 300 ms white-statue hit reaction, and kill-moment text piling onto itself. Onboarding is the biggest gap for the owner's "young children" goal — the anchored tour evaporates after two beats in every fresh-walk capture and 50–60-word tip cards take over.

## Keep (strengths)
- Type system and card language (Cinzel-style headers, ember-orange primary, bevelled dice) — 000/002/003/009/019.
- 3D-shaded dice with spent/MAX/selected states and per-shape d4/d8/d12 glyphs — 021/040.
- Kill choreography skeleton: white-hot freeze → ash burst → floating "+5 EMBERS — EXACT!" — right beats, wrong timing/placement.
- Anchored tour architecture (real GlobalKeys, held bands, ≤12-word copy budget in `tour_overlay.dart`) — the two beats that do show are exactly right for children.
- Settings screen: reduce-motion tri-state, blood toggle, analytics off by default.
- Hero delver sprites (22 articulated, `004_character`) are the best art in the game.

## Ranked issues (impact ÷ effort)

**C0-01 · P1 · animation · S/M — Foes strike from where they stand.** In all four `C_anim_*_enemy-turn` strips the foe's x-position at 028 equals 020 and the "-9" already floats at 030; wind-up is only a brightness/heat pulse. Fix: 120–160 ms dash (use the existing `run` row in `sprite_meta.json`) to a strike mark ~40 dp from the hero, 1 contact frame with 2-frame squash, 200 ms ease-back; wisps dart, brutes hop, maws lunge low. Accept: strip shows foe centroid ≥ 90 px @2x closer to hero on the contact frame for all 4 foes; reduced-motion path keeps a 2-frame lean only.

**C0-02 · P1 · onboarding · M — Guided tour dies after beat 2; text-wall tips take over (verify).** B_fresh_walk 320/360/412: "1 of 5" (04), "2 of 5" (05_pick), then no overlay, no counter, and a 30-word "ROLL, THEN SPEND" ContextTip (05_spend_null), then nothing for intent (05_intent_null). Harness taps every GestureDetector until `active != tour_pick` — SKIP is a GestureDetector, so this may be a harness artefact; either way the fallback is the old card wall. Fix: make the die tap the only live target during `pick` (SKIP behind a 2-tap or hold), ensure `spend`/`intent`/`reroll` beats present, and suppress `ContextTips.rollSpend`/`intentFair` while `tourSeenVersion` is fresh. Accept: fresh-walk plates at 3 widths show "3 of 5", "4 of 5", "5 of 5" cards with ≤12 words; `tour_test.dart` covers skip-not-triggered-by-die-tap.

**C0-03 · P1 · gameplay feedback/layout · S — Kill-moment readout stacks on itself.** 008: "+5 EMBERS — EXACT!" overprints the "-2" damage number and the still-live "⊕7" intent badge; 016: "-6" sits on the "🛡11" badge of a foe at 0/19 whose HP bar still shows a grey stub; 021/040: "OVERKILL +3 → NEXT FOE" and "+5 EMBERS — EXACT!" overprint "ENEMY HP · TURN". Fix: one vertical toast lane above the enemy HP card with 90 ms stagger; hide intent badge and drain ghost bar the moment HP hits 0; damage number anchored to foe centroid, toast to card bottom. Accept: kill plates for 3 foes with zero overlapping text bounds (golden test on text rects).

**C0-04 · P1 · art · M — Enemy sprites are a different game from the hero.** Idle strips: slag brute = brown rectangle with red band, flue crawler = beige "∩", wisp = red square; 16-px 0x72-style sources (`source_base: imp/tiny_zombie`) at 3× with 2-px black outlines; hero is 32×60 with no outline, ~1.7× taller on screen than a "Brute". Fix (one iteration): raise foe render scale so 1 source px = 1 hero px, render at the same pixel density, drop or thin outlines to 1 px rim-light in the palette, and replace the three tutorial-floor foes (flue crawler, cinder wisp, scoria tick) with 32-px CC0 sprites (attribution in CREDITS) or originals. Accept: side-by-side plate at 360 where foe pixel size equals hero pixel size; a parent can name each of the three first foes from silhouette.

**C0-05 · P1 · animation · S — Player hit reaction is a frozen white statue.** C_*_enemy-turn 032–038: hero fully white for 4 sampled frames (~280–320 ms) with an unchanging red slash decal; body pose identical across 032–038; no recoil.
