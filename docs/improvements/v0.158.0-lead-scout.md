# v0.158.0 lead scout — The Shieldwright

Trail: the open eleventh-delver lane × the v0.155 Deep Mark arc.
Archetype audit of the roster: baseline, tank, variance, small kit,
economy, control, swarm, temper-specialist, giant, healer — no kit
LEADS with defense-as-rune (the warden tanks with HP + relic, not
marks), and no kit anywhere touches the new deep tier.

Choices:
- Aegis II on the 6: the deep mark on the TOP face means the best
  roll defends grandest — the emberdelve way of making a shield feel
  earned, not passive. On a low face it would read as a worse mender.
- Three plain d6s, no relic: the kindler's silhouette with one die
  changed — the cleanest possible statement that the MARK is the kit.
- 26 HP: sweep landed in band at the first guess (the ~2-HP-per-
  advantage prior held: one deep face ≈ 4 HP below kindler's 30).

Rejected:
- A deep SURGE lead (runesmith II) — same rune as the runesmith reads
  as a strict upgrade, orphaning the 900-ember chair.
- startRelic iron_scale on top — sweep would need HP down near 22 and
  the fiction muddies (shield rune + shield relic says one thing twice).
- A new 'shield' die id — new dice resize shop pools and re-anchor
  goldens (tinker precedent, v0.50.0).
