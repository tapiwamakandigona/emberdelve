# v0.138.0 lead scout — The Delver's Dice

Trail: the per-delver dressing audit. v0.115 declared 'each delver
keeps their own' for dyes/titles/names/vistas; skins were the one
cosmetic left global — an inconsistency a player meets the moment
they buy a second skin (the shelf grew to 9 in v0.128, so second
skins are now common).

Choices:
- Wear in the picker, buy on the shelf: two verbs, two homes —
  merging them would put a purchase button inside the dressing
  room (rejected by the one-purchase-path ethic).
- Fallback to activeDieSkin, not defaultDieSkin: a player's old
  global choice keeps meaning exactly what it meant (the v0.66
  selectedEpithet precedent).
- No new unlock content: this is a wiring release; the shelf's
  catalog is untouched.
