# v0.47.0 — "The Answered Blow" (response-puzzle enemies)

**Lead:** retention research §7 (docs/research/retention-hooks-2026-08-23.md):
"enemy variety that demands specific responses keeps the core loop from
becoming math homework — future content drops should add *response puzzles*,
not stat sponges." Every intent today is attack / block / attack_block: the
correct answer is always "block the number, spend the rest on attack." This
release adds two intent kinds whose correct answers are *different from that*,
plus three enemies built around them.

## New intent kinds (deterministic, legible, zero RNG in resolution)

### 1. `charge` — burst check ("interrupt it or eat it")
`Intent('charge', bigHit, breakThreshold)` (third positional reuses the
`block` slot; serialized as `threshold`).

- While a charge is telegraphed, HP damage dealt to the enemy this player
  turn is tracked (`hp_lost_this_turn`; block-absorbed damage does NOT count,
  burn/thorns tick after resolution and do not count).
- The moment the tracked total reaches `threshold`, the intent flips to
  `{'kind':'stagger','amount':0}` live and a `charge_broken` event fires —
  the badge updates instantly, the payoff is visible.
- At end of turn: an unbroken `charge` resolves exactly like an `attack` of
  `amount` (blockable as normal); `stagger` resolves as nothing
  (`enemy_staggered` event). Pattern index advances normally either way.

Puzzle: burst now vs. turtle. Big hit is sized so full-blocking is usually
worse than breaking — but a bad roll can make blocking the right call.

### 2. `counter` — riposte stance ("one clean blow, not a flurry")
`Intent('counter', perStrike)`.

- While shown, every die assigned to **attack** that does not kill the enemy
  costs the player `perStrike` damage immediately (player block absorbs
  first; `counter_struck` event; player death checked → loss).
- A killing blow resolves the win BEFORE the counter — an exact big hit is
  the clean answer (synergizes with the exact-kill ember bonus).
- At end of turn the stance simply passes: the enemy does not attack that
  turn (the counter WAS its action).

Puzzle: inverts the combo incentive (many small dice = many counters).
Rewards saving one big die, echo/forge planning, or a block-and-wait turn.

## UI

- `_IntentBadge` (badges.dart): `charge` = flash icon + amount chip plus a
  "break: N" chip; `counter` = riposte icon + per-strike amount; `stagger` =
  muted "—". Spoken/semantics strings state the response in plain words.
- `_explainIntent` (combat_screen.dart) long-press copy for the new kinds.
- `stage.dart` sway: charge sways like an attack.
- `tips.dart onIntent`: charge counts as a big-hit warning trigger; one new
  contextual tip each for first-seen charge and counter.
- Controller choreography: `counter_struck` shows player damage feedback;
  `charge_broken` / `enemy_staggered` get a stage flash/toast.

## Autoplay (bands must stay honest)

Greedy bot learns the minimum: vs `charge` it prefers attacking to break
(blocks only if breaking is out of reach); vs `counter` it blocks with small
dice and attacks only with its single biggest die (or lethal). This models a
player who read the badge — the same floor the bot provides everywhere else.
