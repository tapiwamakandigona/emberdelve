# v0.54.0 — The Epitaph (design)

## What

The Delver's Card (v0.34.0) gains the run's story: a one-or-two-sentence
epitaph under the name line. The Obituary's second act — v0.51.0 put the
story on the summary screen and made it copyable as text; this puts it on
the IMAGE, the artifact that actually travels on WhatsApp statuses and
stories. The card stops being a scoreboard and becomes a story.

## Why now

Scouted and deferred twice (v0.52, v0.53) for surface fatigue; now one
full release of distance from v0.51.0. Hook logic: players paste the
obituary text in chats — the image-first channels were the missing half.

## Shape

- New pure `epitaphLine(...)` in lib/game/obituary.dart — the CARD-SIZED
  cut, not the full obituary. The card already states mode, embers, and
  the floor trace; the epitaph restates none of it. It carries only the
  narrative the numbers can't: how the run ended, and who ended it.
  - Win:  "<who> came back up." / "<who> walked out with the Ember."
          + " <Boss> felled at the bottom."
  - Loss: "Here fell <who> — <killer> ended the run on floor N." /
          "The delve took <who> — …". Killer-less legacy record: the
          sentence ends at the floor, no killer invented.
  - Deterministic on the run seed (same parity trick as obituaryText),
    losses dignified, every name a banked fact.
- Controller getter `delveEpitaphLine` mirrors `delveStoryText`'s fact
  gathering — the card and the summary can never disagree.
- `DelverCardFacts.epitaph` ('' default keeps every existing const-facts
  test valid); rendered italic/dim under the name line, key
  `card-epitaph`; included in the clipboard fallback text.

## Rejected

- Full `obituaryText` on the card: duplicates the mode/embers lines the
  card already shows, and 3 sentences at accessibility scale risks the
  fixed 340×420 frame.
- Mid-sentence ellipsis clamp: dishonest artifact. The short VARIANT is
  complete by construction.

## Found and fixed on the way

The shipped card already overflowed its fixed 340×420 frame by 90px at
1.3x device text scale (worst realistic facts) — big-text devices were
exporting a broken card since v0.34.0. Fix: the card is an exported
image, so it renders under `MediaQuery.withNoTextScaling` (a picture
doesn't reflow with system text; it ships at 3x and the preview sheet
around it keeps honoring the device setting). The epitaph's worst-case
test is what surfaced it.

## Gates

- Zero sim changes by construction (pure string + UI only).
- test/epitaph_test.dart pins exact strings, live win/loss facts (pinned
  seeds 1/18 easy kindler), card render/omission, ethics sweep, and the
  worst-realistic-string 1.3x-scale overflow gate (the overflow probe
  never opens the share sheet, so the card guards itself — test font
  wraps wider than shipped fonts, same lesson as the boon header).
- Plates: card sheet win + loss at 360×640, 412×915, 800×1280,
  320×570@1.3x.
