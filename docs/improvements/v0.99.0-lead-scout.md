# v0.99.0 lead scout — The Colored Card

Lead (queued as "share-card polish"): audited every identity signal a
profile carries against what the shared card states. Sprite, dye,
given name, epithet, epitaph, delve code — all on the card. Vista was
the one gap, fresh off v0.98.0 adding a seventh.

Choices:
- Color only, no caption ("seen under the Moonveil" was drafted and
  cut — the card is already ten lines, and the wash IS the statement).
- Reused Art.backgroundWash(0, id) instead of new per-card colors: one
  source of truth, and depth 0 keeps strata out of a depthless
  artifact.
- Current-vista-not-banked follows the dye precedent exactly.

Rejected: grading the whole card through the vista ColorFilter matrix
(tints text and accent hues — the win-gold must stay win-gold);
per-run vista banking (new save field for a cosmetic; dye precedent
says no).

Queued leads: more hearth tales past the first cycle; a Ledger filter
by delver.
