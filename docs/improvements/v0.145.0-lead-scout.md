# v0.145.0 lead scout — The Bearer

Trail: archetype audit. Balanced / tank / variance / sharp /
economy / control / swarm / smith — the empty pole was the GIANT:
fewest dice, biggest die, the flintwright mirrored.

Choices:
- Pure data (existing dice, existing relic, runesmith's
  startTempers schema): no sim change, no re-anchor. Iron Scale
  reuse is the tinker precedent — a NEW relic resizes the shop
  pool and re-anchors every golden.
- Echo on the 12 over blade/aegis: echo makes the single grand
  roll TEACH the other die — coverage, not just damage, which is
  exactly what two dice lack.
- The sweep story is the release: four kit iterations documented,
  and the dice-count prior (~30 points a die) recorded for the
  tenth delver's designer.
