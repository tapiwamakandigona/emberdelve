# v0.48.0 — The Iron Between (design)

## One improvement

Elite fights get their own battle theme. Until now `_bossFight` counted
`elite == true`, so every elite borrowed the crowned-boss piece ("The Deep
Crowned") — which diluted the boss moment and wasted a free stakes signal.
Now the ladder is audible: **combat → combat_elite → boss_combat**.

## Why this hook

- Our one outside review (2026-08-23) scored the music 9/10 and praised the
  boss designs — audio is earned credit worth spending, same argument that
  carried v0.45.0 "The Deeper Song".
- Retention research (docs/research/retention-hooks-2026-08-23.md §7): keep
  the loop from becoming math homework by making *stakes readable*. A theme
  shift on elite contact is stakes you hear before the first die lands.
- The Gramophone gains an eighth record — collection pull for free, earned
  the honest way (cross blades with an elite), replayable at the hearth.
- Boss music becomes boss-only again, so "The Deep Crowned" regains its
  meaning. Existing profiles keep any boss_combat record they earned via
  elites (historically honest — they did hear it).

## Track

Kevin MacLeod, **"Volatile Reaction"** (incompetech.com, CC BY 4.0) — driving
percussion between "Curse of the Scarab" (combat) and "Five Armies" (boss) in
intensity. Family recipe: measured gain to −19 LUFS (verified −19.06, TP
−9.17 dBFS), alimiter −1.4 dBFS ceiling, 20/40 ms edge fades, OGG q4 stereo
44.1 kHz, 165 s loop, 2.4 MB (in family: siblings run 1.5–3.5 MB).

CREDITS.md directly).

## Mechanics

- `AudioService.musicPaths['combat_elite']`; `musicKeyForPhase` takes
  `eliteFight`; **boss outranks elite** (a crowned foe that is also flagged
  elite plays boss music).
- `GameController._bossFight` is now boss-only; new `_eliteFight` getter;
  both passed to `syncPhase` and the heardTracks recorder (same
  gameplay-owned static rule as ever, testable with audio == null).
- Gramophone catalog: "Iron Between", hint "Cross blades with an elite foe.",
  inserted between Steel and Ember and Deeper Still (journey order — elites
  appear before the midpoint on most maps).
- Backgrounds unchanged on purpose: elites keep the boss backdrop
  (game_root.dart) — the visual language of "big fight" still reads; only the
  score now distinguishes iron from crown.

## Deliberately NOT included

- No sim changes (simVersion 7 untouched); no re-anchor needed.
- No per-elite themes; one piece keeps the signal clean and the APK small.
- No purchase/timer unlock — the record is earned in play (spec §Ethics).
