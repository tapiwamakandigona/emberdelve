# v0.116.0 lead scout — The Spoken Dice

Trail: after v0.114.0's re-anchor bill, the queue wanted content
with ZERO seeded-run surface — codex growth is exactly that (meta
layer, no sim streams touched). Dice are the game's central fiction
("a bag of small promises", place:the_dice) and the last major
catalog without its own section.

Choices:
- Five base cuts, not 30 dice: one story per SHAPE; variants are
  the shape re-promised. Coverage-for-coverage's-sake reads as
  filler and dilutes the sink.
- No coverage guard against diceOrder (unlike enemies/relics):
  partial-by-design needs the exact-list pin instead, plus a
  mod-free pin proving each ref is the plain cut.
- Section closes the book (world → company → enemies → relics →
  dice): tools last, like an appendix.
- PLATE LESSON: sections deep in a long ListView need a real scroll
  budget (600 px × maxScrolls 400) — the clone's default 200×40
  stalls silently ("Bad state: No element" from dragUntilVisible).
