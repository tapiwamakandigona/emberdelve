# v0.129.0 lead scout — The Earned Titles

Trail: the reward-tier audit. A milestone lands hardest when every
reward tier answers it — the tales arc has vista+achievements, the
doubled week has vista+achievements+epithet-adjacent recap. The
temper and rotation arcs (v0.124–0.127) had honors and a vista but
no TITLE — and epithets are the most social tier (worn on shared
cards).

Choices:
- the Tempered at 10, not 25: titles mark arrival, badges mark
  accumulation — the title sits at the vista's rung, Well Tempered
  keeps the long road.
- 'the Weathered' over any counted name: promise-worded unlocks
  get count-free display names (the Many-Handed doctrine) so a
  rotation growth never forces a rename.
- Rejected: a delve-code epithet ('the Traded') — codes have no
  banked counter, and inventing one for a title inverts the
  honesty contract (counters exist for play, titles read them).
