# v0.20.0 "The Living Ladder" — ascension rebalance

## The problem, measured

`tool/ascension_sweep_probe.dart` (100 seeds × character × rung, hard):

| rung | kindler | warden | gambler | ascetic | median loss floor |
|------|---------|--------|---------|---------|-------------------|
| A1   | 31%     | 46%    | 27%     | 23%     | 3–4 |
| A3   | 14%     | 25%    | 12%     | 10%     | 3 |
| A5   | 5%      | 10%    | 5%      | 3%      | 3 |
| A8   | 2%      | 2%     | 2%      | 0%      | 3 |
| A12+ | 0%      | 0%     | 0%      | 0%      | 3 |

Two failures:

1. **The ladder dead-ends.** Rung N+1 unlocks only by *winning* at rung N
   (controller.dart:878). With 0% at A12+ for every character, rungs
   ~13–20 are unreachable in practice — yet the game sells "20 rungs"
   (forge_sheet.dart) and awards "Reach ascension 20" (achievements.dart).
   A promise the math can't keep is a §Ethics problem, not just tuning.
2. **It's a bouncer, not a climb.** Median loss floor is 3 at every rung —
   the flat `+rung` attack bonus front-loads all the difficulty onto the
   first fights. This is the exact failure v0.3.3 fixed for hard mode
   ("68% of all hard losses on the very first fight").

## The fix

Replace the flat `+rung` with a **layer-scaled ramp**, the same shape as
hard's staircase (deterministic pure function, no RNG):

```
ascensionAttackBonus(rung, layer) = ceil(rung / d)
  d = 7  layers ≤ 3   (door stays open: A20 adds only +3 early)
  d = 4  layers 4–6   (A20 adds +5 mid)
  d = 3  layers 7+ & boss (A20 adds +7 late — the bite lives here)
```

Applied to attack amounts and to `attack_block` block bonuses, stacking
with the hard delta exactly as before. Rung 0 stays byte-identical to
today (golden anchor untouched — the anchor pins normal/A0).

## Acceptance criteria (bot = lower bound on humans)

1. Win rate strictly falls as rung climbs (existing monotonicity gate).
2. The door opens: median loss floor ≥ 4 at every rung (climb, not bouncer).
3. The ladder is provably alive: at least one bot-won seed exists at A20
   (seed hunt) — if the weak bot can win one, a strong human can climb.
4. A1 lands near current A1 (it should feel like the first rung, not a cliff).

Tuning is empirical: sweep, adjust divisors, re-sweep. Numbers below are
the shipped result, not the first guess.

## Compatibility

- No simVersion bump (v0.3.3 / v0.17.0 precedent): snapshot shape is
  unchanged, and mid-run saves bake enemy pattern amounts at
  `combatBegin`, so a resumed fight keeps its old numbers; only *new*
  fights use the new ramp.
- Replays/traces: rung-0 runs (all shares/dailies/weeklies at A0) are
  byte-identical. Ascension seed-challenges re-delved across versions may
  diverge — same accepted trade as every balance pass.

## Not in scope

- Per-rung distinct modifiers (Slay-the-Spire-style ascension menu) — a
  content-design project, not a balance fix.
- Ascension-specific rewards/cosmetics.
- Easy/normal ascension curves get the same function (it is a pure
  function of rung and layer, difficulty-agnostic), but tuning targets
  hard, the ladder's home.

## Shipped result (measured)

Final shape: attack ramp as above **plus** enemy HP ×(1 + 0.01·rung) so
every rung changes the fight (the attack ramp only steps at divisor
boundaries; +2%/rung was tried first and re-killed ascetic A20 to 0%).

Sweep after (100 seeds × char × rung, hard):

| rung | kindler | warden | gambler | ascetic |
|------|---------|--------|---------|---------|
| A1   | 31%     | 45%    | 26%     | 22%     |
| A5   | 27%     | 37%    | 22%     | 16%     |
| A8   | 18%     | 27%    | 14%     | 13%     |
| A12  | 15%     | 23%    | 12%     | 9%      |
| A16  | 8%      | 14%    | 8%      | 5%      |
| A20  | 5%      | 13%    | 8%      | 3%      |

Acceptance check: (1) monotonic decline ✓; (2) A20 loss histogram spreads
floor 2→9 with deaths at the boss (worst single floor 38% vs the old
bouncer's 68%-on-first-fight) — median stays 3 because A20 is a hard
door, but it is no longer *only* a door; humans reach A20 having won
A19, bots arrive cold; (3) bot-won seeds exist at A20 for every
character (pinned in test/ascension_ladder_test.dart) ✓; (4) A1 within
1pt of pre-change A1 for all characters ✓.
