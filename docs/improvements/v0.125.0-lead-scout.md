# v0.125.0 lead scout — The Tempered Hand

Trail: the v0.124.0 wrap — every combat system that matters has a
lifetime arc (kills, exact kills, bosses, provings, hard wins), and
the newly-content-bearing temper system had none. Meta visibility
is what makes a mechanic feel FIRST-CLASS: a player who tempers
every run had nothing in the Ledger to show for it.

Choices:
- Banked from run['tempers_used'] at run end, not incremented at
  the temper command: the sim stays meta-free (the architecture
  line), and an abandoned run banks nothing — consistent with
  every other counter.
- Win-or-lose banking (charRuns precedent, not charWins): the
  temper is spent forge work, not an outcome.
- 25 for Well Tempered: one temper per delve max → 25 ≈ a
  committed habit across a month of play, in line with Old
  Delver's 50-run horizon.
