# v0.135.0 lead scout — The Runesmith

Trail: the roster audit against the mechanic map. Since the
seventh delver (v0.118) the game grew a whole temper arc (axes,
depth, collection, vista) — but no delver EXPRESSES it: every
archetype (balance/tank/risk/few-dice/gold/forge/swarm) predates
the anvil. New mechanics earn a face; a delver is the strongest
retention surface the roster has (variety + a 900-ember sink).

Choices:
- startTempers as DATA on CharacterDef, not sim special-casing:
  the schema comment IS the contract (smith's work vs player's
  work), and a ninth delver can reuse it.
- Surge on the d8's top face, not blade: blade would be raw power
  creep; surge is tempo identity (roll the 8, get a reroll back)
  that rewards play, not stats.
- HP 26 from the sweep, not vibes: the first setting landed in
  band — recorded so the next delver starts from '2 HP per
  meaningful kit advantage' as a prior.
