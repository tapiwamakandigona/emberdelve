# v0.113.0 lead scout — The Cold Honors

Trail: v0.111.0 built the peak, v0.112.0 the trophy; the ledger
record is the third leg studios ship with a rotation peak (badge +
cosmetic + stat), and ours was missing. Cheapest release of the arc:
the counter already existed and was already tested.

Choices:
- Same stat as the vista rather than a second counter: one truth,
  two readers — a divergence between "vista earned" and "honor
  earned" is impossible by construction.
- Targets 1 and 3 (not 1/5/10): doubled weeks come once per 6-week
  cycle; target 3 is ~4 months of play. A ten-target would be a
  year-scale grind — pressure disguised as recognition.
- Names carry the frostvein winter language ('The First Winter',
  'Thrice Wintered') so the arc reads as one story across
  challenge, vista, and honors.
