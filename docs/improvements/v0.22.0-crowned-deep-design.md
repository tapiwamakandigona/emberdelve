# v0.22.0 "The Crowned Deep" — boss content drop (design)

One improvement: the second return-moment content drop, this time aimed at the
one retention lever v0.12.0 deliberately left untouched — **new bosses**. With
v0.21.0 The Watchtower shipped, players finally have an in-game channel that
tells them a release exists; this is the release worth telling them about.

## Why bosses now

- v0.12.0 "New Embers" grew regulars/elites/events/relics but pinned bosses at
  6 specifically to avoid a golden re-anchor. That was the right call then
  (two re-anchors in one week would have been reckless); the cost is that the
  run's *climax* has not changed since v0.5.0.
- Studio research (docs/improvements/studio-priorities): the return moment
  players cite most in genre postmortems is "there's a new boss". Daily Trials
  share a seed → the day the drop lands, everyone's daily can crown a new boss.
- Boss variety is a pure function of seed (`bossForSeed = seed % count`), so
  the sim change is data-only: **no simVersion bump** (pool contents, not
  snapshot shape) — v0.12.0 precedent holds.

## Content (all data-only, appended at band END, never reordered)

Two new bosses with rhythms not yet in the vocabulary (existing six: 4-beat
mixed, slow tank, pure race, 5-beat teacher,always-guard pressure plate, double
tap). Boss band stays hp 94–112, swings 21–36 — fairness over spectacle:

1. `slag_sovereign` — **the drawbridge**: `block 30, block 24, attack 41`.
   Two guarded beats, then the single biggest telegraphed swing in the game.
   Rewards counting and burst timing; punishes even damage dripped into guard.
   hp ~108.
2. `hearthless_king` — **the pendulum**: `attack 34, block 31`. The only
   strict 2-beat metronome in the roster: every other beat is free, but the
   hits are the largest *regular-cadence* swings we ship. Simple to read,
   brutal to out-tempo. hp ~100.

Supporting deep-band content so the road to the new kings also changes:
- 2 late regulars (layer 7+): `ashglass_sentinel` (long guard-then-spike),
  `coal_seam_wyrm` (attack_block weave) — exact stats tuned in-band vs the
  v0.17.0 Even Scales measurements.
- 2 events: one boss-lore event per new boss (decline last, per lib
  convention).
- 2 relics: legal hooks only; one anti-guard-leaning
  (fits drawbridge counterplay), one tempo relic.
- Codex entries for all 8 new ids. Sprites: palette variants
  (tool/gen_variant_sprites.py) — sovereign←ashen_colossus,
  king←ember_tyrant; PROVENANCE.md rows; existing sheets must regenerate
  byte-identical (determinism re-proof).

## Golden re-anchor (deliberate, the third, procedure documented)

`bossForSeed` indexes by `seed % 8` after the drop, so every seed can remap.
- Anchor seed 20260723 ≡ 3 (mod 8) → maps to **cinder_hierophant** (was
  ember_tyrant). content_test's pinned anchor boss and the enemies.dart
  ordering comment are updated to say so, with this doc as the recorded
  reason. The pin's *purpose* (force deliberate re-anchors) is preserved.
- New `bossAnchorSeeds`: consecutive seeds **20260720..20260727 ≡ 0..7
  (mod 8)** hit each boss once in list order (extends the existing
  20260722..27 scheme downward). Every one of the six old anchor seeds maps
  to a different boss under mod 8 (e.g. 20260722: ashen_colossus →
  pyre_matriarch), so all six remap — record old→new seed/boss/golden.
- All 9 goldens (goldenV6 successor + 8 boss anchors) measured **twice per
  seed** from real runs, old → new recorded in sim_test comment + progress.md.
  Never edit a single pin to green.
- `expect(bosses, equals(6))` → `equals(8)` in content_test.
- test/ascension_ladder_test.dart pins A20 bot-won seeds per character; boss
  remap can flip those runs → re-hunt with tool/asc_seed_hunt_test.dart and
  re-pin, recording old → new seeds.
