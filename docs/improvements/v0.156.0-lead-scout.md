# v0.156.0 lead scout — The Deep Day

Trail: v0.155.0 shipped the Deep Mark and closed with "a Deep Mark-aware
trial" as an open lane. This release is that trial, kept to the standing
trial pattern: one goal kind, one catalog entry, append-LAST.

Choices:
- `deep_marks_at_least` over reusing `tempers_at_least` with param 2: two
  tempers on DIFFERENT faces is breadth, not depth — the trial's whole point
  is to pose the rest-fire question with a declared answer. Counting tiers
  in the die records is the only honest reading.
- goalParam 1 is the only sensible value: with the temper cap at 2 a run
  can hold at most one deep mark, so any higher param would be a lie the
  banned-words sweep can't catch.
- Judge reads `run['custom_dice']` tiers with the absent-key = tier-1
  contract, mirroring resolveRunDie without importing the sim into the
  game layer — the sealed-sim contract stays intact.
- Uniformity floor made proportional (60% of fair share) over bumping the
  fixed 80 to some new fixed number: every append changes the modulus and
  reshuffles the histogram, so any fixed pin goes stale on the next append.
  This was a latent stale-test bug this release happened to expose.
- Rejected this pass: a deep-mark ACHIEVEMENT (the honors catalog is
  closed-ish and the trial already gives the mechanic a calendar presence);
  a deep-mark mutator day (deepening is player choice at a rest fire — a
  mutator can't impose it without touching the sim).

Open lanes next: eleventh delver, hearth themes (14, room to grow), more
out-of-run tips. Closed lanes unchanged.
