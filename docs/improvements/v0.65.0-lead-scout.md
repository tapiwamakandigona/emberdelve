# v0.65.0 — The Charted Depth (lead scout)

## The lead

The picker's tally (v0.60.0) tells you your record with each delver — wins
and delves — but not the fact players actually brag about: **how deep that
character has gone**. The global mark exists (`bestFloor`, v0.5.0, announced
by v0.61.0's Deepest Mark line), but it is one number for the whole profile.
A player who mains the kindler and dabbles in the gambler has two different
stories, and the picker tells neither.

## Evidence

- Balatro-style stats culture (Template Foundry write-up, used for
  v0.57.0): per-character records are the most-screenshotted stat panel —
  a per-slot depth is identity, not telemetry.
- Antihero Studios retry data (80.5% retry after loss): a per-character
  record gives a *local* goal after a loss with a weaker delver — "the
  peddler's floor 3" is beatable tonight even when the profile record
  (floor 9) is not.
- Our own review (2026-08-23): "You could add character customisation" —
  identity-per-delver is the thread; depth-per-delver is the cheapest
  honest cut of it, with zero art budget.

## The cut

`charBestFloor: Map<String, int>` on MetaState — deepest 1-based layer
each character ever stood on, won or lost (exact same fact as `bestFloor`,
charted per delver).

- **Banked** in `_bankRun` beside `bestFloor`, from `floorReached` — never
  derived, never estimated.
- **Seeded** for pre-v0.65.0 saves from `runHistory` (per-character max of
  provable record floors) — the same honesty rule as bestFloor's v0.5.0
  seeding. A save WITH the key never re-seeds.
- **Cloud merge**: per-key MAX (`_maxMap`, same convention as
  charRuns/charWins).
- **Shown** on the picker tally: `5 wins · 12 delves · floor 6`. No
  charted depth → the plain v0.60.0 tally; never a guessed floor, no "floor
  0" clutter.

## What this is NOT

- Not a sim change (zero movement; pinned seeds and goldens stand).
- Not a new screen (rejected: chronicle stats page).
- Not shown for locked or never-delved characters (v0.60.0's no-shame rule
  carries over).
