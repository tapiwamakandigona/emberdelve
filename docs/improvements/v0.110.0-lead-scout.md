# v0.110.0 lead scout — The Named Company

Trail: the 8/23 review loved the characters and music and asked
"what's a delve?" — v0.104.0 answered the world; the roster's own
stories were the open half. Player attachment research (and the
review itself) says characters are the hook people name first, and
lore is the cheapest durable deepener: write once, no balance risk,
pure ember sink demand.

Choices:
- Codex 'delver' kind over a picker-screen lore tab: the Codex is
  already the book of the world, has the unseal economy, and the
  'place' kind (v0.104.0) proved the generic-kind seam.
- Names from characters.dart, not stored: the enemies/relics
  anti-lying rule, third application.
- 15 embers (common-lore price): the company is dearer than the
  world (10) but not elite (20).
- Each story encodes the delver's mechanical identity so flavor
  teaches without paywalling rules (charter line held).
- delve_itself_test's codexEntries[8] index pin would have silently
  kept passing while testing the wrong thing — made kind-robust.

Considered and deferred: 'double week' composed weekly rules (wide
seam — weeklyMutator is a single-String label through saves, top
bar, share text; needs its own release with save-compat care).
