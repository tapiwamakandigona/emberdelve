# v0.150.0 lead scout — The Mender

Trail: v0.150 milestone number → the big lane (tenth delver).
Archetype audit of the roster: baseline, tank, variance, small
kit, economy, relics, swarm, tempers, giant — no healer. The mend
rune (v0.124) existed for player forging but no kit led with it.

Choices:
- Mend on the WORST face (the d8's 1): the healer archetype done
  the emberdelve way — no sustain engine, just the bad roll
  paying something. A mend on a high face would compete with the
  face's own value; on the 1 it converts pure loss.
- No relic: bearer needed steel because two dice starve intent
  coverage; three dice don't. The mark alone tested strong even
  at 26 HP.
- 24 HP ties flintwright for the roster's smallest frame — the
  fiction (menders patch others, rarely themselves) and the
  sweep agree.
