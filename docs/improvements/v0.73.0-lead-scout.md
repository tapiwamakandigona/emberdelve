# v0.73.0 lead scout — The Opened Vista

## Question
The wardrobe announces itself: v0.68.0 gave every earned epithet a gold
line on the summary. Vistas — the OTHER earned cosmetic, the reviewer's
"change backgrounds" — unlock in total silence. A player who fells their
15th distinct enemy is never told the verdigris now stands open; they'd
have to wander into the delver screen and notice a swatch lit. Symmetry
says: announce the fact, once, in the same breath as the run result.

## Lead — announce newly opened vistas on the summary
- Same derived-diff shape as v0.68.0: snapshot `vistasOrder.where(vistaUnlocked)`
  BEFORE banking, diff after every counter has moved, stash in transient
  `pendingVistas` (cleared by resetRun like every pending announcement).
- One gold line per vista, key `opened-vista-<id>`:
  `'The <Name> vista stands open.'` Pure fact about a thing already
  earned; where to wear it is the delver screen's business (§Ethics —
  no teaser, no button).
- Nothing persists, nothing merges, nothing can double-fire: the diff
  lives inside one bank pass, and unlocks are pure functions of counters
  that only rise.

## Rejected
- **"N more marks to the duskquartz" teaser** — next-goal bait on a loss
  screen; FOMO-shaped like the rejected nearest-mark teaser (v0.68.0).
- **Toast/interstitial** — the summary's quiet-line inventory is the
  house style; a modal for a background is noise.
- **Announcing on the title screen instead** — the moment of earning is
  the bank; a later ambush loses the "same breath" recognition and needs
  persisted seen-state (the summary diff needs none).
- **Retroactive announcements for already-open vistas** — would fire six
  stale lines on veterans' next runs; the diff-only shape can't.
