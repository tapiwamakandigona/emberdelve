# v0.16.0 — "The Still Flame" (design)

One improvement (DEMAND): a **Reduce motion** comfort setting — screen
shake, drifting ember particles, and damage-pop arcs quiet down for
players who need a steadier image. Defaults to the OS accessibility
setting; one visible toggle overrides it either way.

## Why (studio lesson 4 — trust; industry accessibility practice)

- Post-launch accessibility passes are a consistent studio investment
  (Celeste's Assist Mode, God of War Ragnarök's 60+ options, the AbleGamers
  APX guidance on motion). Vestibular discomfort from screen shake is one
  of the most common complaints — and one of the cheapest to respect.
- Our combat feel leans on shake (design-system: "bigger hits shake
  harder") + full-screen EmberDrift. Great for most, genuinely unpleasant
  for some. A player who feels sick doesn't churn loudly — they just leave.
- Trust framing: respecting the OS-level `disableAnimations` flag without
  being asked is exactly the quiet-competence trust our charter sells.

## Shape

1. `AudioSettings` grows `String reduceMotion` — `'system'` (default) /
   `'on'` / `'off'`. Persisted in emberdelve_settings.json like the rest;
   absent key ⇒ `'system'` (old files keep working, no migration).
   Effective value: `'system'` reads `MediaQuery.disableAnimations`.
2. A tiny static resolver `Motion.reduced` (set once per frame from the
   root builder's MediaQuery + live settings; same pattern as
   AudioService.applySettings) so leaf FX never need context plumbing:
   - `ShakeBoxState.shake()` → no-op when reduced.
   - `EmberDrift` → renders NOTHING when reduced (static sparks read as
     dead pixels; absence is calmer than freeze).
   - `DamagePop` → number appears and fades in place (information kept,
     arc + pop scale removed).
   - Combat hit flash/vignette pulses stay — sub-100ms opacity cues, not
     motion; removing them would hide information.
3. Settings: new COMFORT section above ABOUT — three-way selector
   (System / Reduced / Full), key `'reduce-motion'`, honest caption
   "Steadies the screen: no shake, no drifting embers." Haptics toggle
   moves under COMFORT too (it always was comfort, not audio).
4. No gameplay/sim contact; purely presentation. No telemetry.

## Non-goals

- No per-effect toggles (three-way is enough; more = option soup).
- No colorblind pass this release (needs its own research + palette work).
- No animation-speed slider.
