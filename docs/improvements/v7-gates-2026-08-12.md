# The v7 gates — what the Face Forge had to survive before shipping

Iteration 08. New mechanics are cheap to write and expensive to trust. These
are the checks v7 had to pass, and the two bugs they caught.

## Migration: discard, don't guess

`test/v7_migration_test.dart`. A v6 autosave is **discarded** at boot, not
migrated. There is no v6 representation of a tempered die, so any migration
would be inventing a pool the player never chose. What the tests hold to:

- a v6-stamped snapshot boots to no run, no crash, profile intact
- a v7 snapshot carrying a tempered die resumes with identical `stateHash`,
  its `custom_dice` entry, and the pool still pointing at `custom_1`
- a run using a rune and a keystone replays byte-identically from the same
  seed, and the replay is asserted to actually contain a `keystone_triggered`
- snapshot/restore mid-combat preserves the per-turn state the new mechanics
  added (`surge_used`, `echo_pending`, `ashen_used`, `bellows_action`), and
  the resumed sim keeps resolving identically from that point

## Sweep: the difficulty curve still slopes the right way

`tool/v7_sweep_probe.dart`, 120 seeds per cell. The bot gained an **opt-in**
temper policy (Blade on the top face of its biggest die) — off by default, so
the long-lived golden replays keep their meaning.

| Powers | easy | normal | hard | asc 0 / 3 / 6 / 12 / 20 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| off | 86.7% | 62.5% | 35.8% | 62.5 / 27.5 / 10.8 / 0 / 0 |
| keystone | 86.7% | 62.5% | 38.3% | 62.5 / 28.3 / 10.8 / 0 / 0 |
| keystone + temper | 86.7% | 62.5% | 37.5% | 62.5 / 25.0 / 10.0 / 0.8 / 0 |

Monotonic in every row, on both axes — asserted, not eyeballed. The powers are
worth a couple of points where the fight is close and nothing where it isn't,
which is the intent: they change *how* a run is played more than whether a
greedy bot survives it. A bot cannot use a rune well; a player can.

## Fuzz: 24,000 commands of nonsense

Randomized command soup — bad die indices, faces off the end of the die,
unknown runes, keystone picks out of range — with hard invariants checked after
**every** command: HP in range, gold non-negative, at most one temper, keystone
cap and no duplicates, every pool id still resolvable, every custom die
carrying a real rune.

**Bug found: a killing blow reported negative HP.** Damage was subtracted
without a floor, so a lethal hit stored −17 and published it in the
`enemy_attacked` event. The HUD prints that value verbatim, and TalkBack reads
"hp: −17 of 40". HP is now floored at zero while `damage` still reports the
full hit, so the number on screen stays honest.

That fix moved exactly one golden — `ashfall_twins`, the one anchor seed whose
run ends in death: `642212611 -> 183009563`, measured twice. The other five
bosses and `goldenV6` are untouched, which is the evidence that the change
reaches death and nothing else.

**Bug found in the fuzz itself.** The first version posted `start_run` at
`run_lost`; a terminal sim accepts nothing, so after the first death it spent
80% of its budget bouncing off a dead sim — 92% rejection, near-zero coverage,
and a green tick. It now builds a fresh sim at terminal, and there is a
coverage gate: every major phase must be visited more than 20 times and the
rejection rate must stay under 60%. A test that stops testing must fail, not
pass quietly.

Second correction, this time to a check that was wrong rather than the code:
negative *enemy* HP is legitimate — that surplus is the overkill splash carry,
and `runPost` clears `combatOver` after every command, so the corpse sits in
state until the next fight begins. Scoped to live fights.

## Perf: the new UI is nearly free

`tool/perf_probe_test.dart`, against the numbers recorded for the 2.5D pass:

| Scenario | before | now |
|---|---|---|
| combat idle | 2.0 paints/frame | 2.0 |
| die tap storm | 19.9 | 20.1 |
| full tap storm | 96.6 | 97.3 |

Under 1% on the interactive paths, nothing at idle. The rune mark is a corner
glyph inside the die's existing repaint boundary, which is why.
