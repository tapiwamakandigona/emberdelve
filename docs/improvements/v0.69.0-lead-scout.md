# v0.69.0 lead scout — The Standing Delver (the delver on their own summary)

Date: 2026-08-26. Scout ran after v0.68.0 (The Earned Name) closed.

## The lead

The summary — the game's most emotional screen, seen after EVERY run — never
shows WHO delved. A generic trophy or flame icon stands where the delver
should. Three releases just built per-delver identity (worn dyes, worn
epithets, earned names announced), and the screen where that identity should
pay off ends the run faceless. Identity investment is the retention thread
the whole wardrobe arc pulls on (Template Foundry post-run stats culture;
the 80.5% retry-after-loss stat rides on the summary feeling personal).

## Shape

- Replace the result icon (trophy/flame) with the run's delver sprite —
  `SpriteView(charId, dye: Art.dyeFilter(meta.dyeFor(charId)))` — standing
  above the headline. Won: upright, full color. Lost: same sprite, dimmed
  (opacity ~0.55) — the dark claimed them, but they're still YOUR delver.
- Under the headline, one small line naming them with their worn title:
  'The Kindler, the Unburnt' (key `summary-delver-name`), gold epithet —
  exactly the picker's voice, resolved via `meta.epithetFor(charId)`. When
  no epithet is worn, the bare name.
- Keep the icon SIZE slot (56dp) so vertical rhythm and short-screen
  overflow behavior don't shift: sprite height 56.
- charId from `run['character']` (same read as the delve-code block).

## Rejected alternatives

- Sprite + weapon + vista backdrop diorama: rejected — the summary is a
  ledger, not a stage; one sprite is the identity note, a diorama is
  clutter and a plate-sweep risk.
- Animating the sprite (bob): ACCEPTED actually — SpriteView(bob:) is the
  established "alive" idiom and costs nothing. Loss keeps bob=false
  (stillness reads as the dignity).
- Removing the trophy/flame entirely from the screen: the icon IS being
  replaced by the sprite; the headline text still carries won/lost. No
  information is lost.

## Risk

Zero sim movement, zero save movement. UI-only → overflow sweep + plates
gate. Tests that pin the summary icon (if any) need re-pinning — grep
`emoji_events` in test/.
