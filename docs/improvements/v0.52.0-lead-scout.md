## Ranked candidates

### 1. The Tinker's Proving (LEADING) — finish what v0.50.0 started

The Tinker shipped as a delver but the surrounding content never learned
its name. Verified gaps:

- **Provings**: exactly 9, characters covered kindler×2, warden×2,
  gambler×2, ascetic×2, peddler×1 — **no tinker proving**
  (lib/data/provings.dart; test/provings_test.dart pins
  `provings.length, 9` at L36 — the test updates with the content).
  A tenth proving needs a **bot-winnable pinned seed hunt** (winnability
  proof at provings_test.dart L61 runs the exact run through autoplay),
  tinker on normal fits the "control archetype" story. Reuse the offline
  hunt harness from tool/tinker_sweep_probe_test.dart.
- **Epithets**: 9, none tinker-scoped (lib/data/epithets.dart). Stat
  vocabulary already supports `char_wins` + param (achievements.dart L68;
  'tinker' param already used by the tinker_wins achievement at L314), so
  "the Well-Oiled" (char_wins/tinker, target N) is pure data.
- Waymarks/summary are stat-generic — no work needed there.

Data-only, zero sim changes, goldens untouched. One release, honest scope:
1 proving + 1–2 epithets + news entry. Effort: small apart from the seed
hunt (bot must WIN the exact proving run — hunt on tinker normal seeds,
the v0.50.0 pin table says seed 1 wins normal already, but a proving wants
its own dedicated seed with a story-worthy map; verify a handful and pick).

### 2. Pre-fight boss "rumor" at run start (carried from v0.51 scout)

The run's boss is seed-determined (`bossForSeed(seed)`, run_layer.dart L63)
— a map-screen whisper ("They say the Bellows waits below") telegraphs the
ending and seeds dread. Presentation-only read of an existing pure
function; needs copy care to avoid spoiler fatigue on retraced/shared
runs. Medium effort (map screen real estate at 320px is tight).

### 3. Obituary follow-through: share the story on the Delver's Card

The v0.51.0 epitaph is plain text; the Card (share_card.dart) could carry
its opening sentence. Small, but two releases in a row on the same
surface risks fatigue — hold unless review feedback asks for it.

## Recommendation

Ship #1 as v0.52.0 "The Tinker's Proving". It closes a real content gap a
player can notice ("every delver has a proving but mine"), is zero-risk to
the sim, and the seed hunt is mechanical with existing tooling.
