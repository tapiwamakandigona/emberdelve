# v0.62.0 lead scout — The Kept Fire

## Research pass (2026-08-25, during GitHub-auth wait)

- Helpshift "Re-Engagement Campaigns for Mobile Games: 2026 Playbook": the
  window of return is the FIRST 90 SECONDS after a lapsed player relaunches
  — they must remember where they were and feel the game kept faith.
- Playio FTUE research (2026-08): returning players and veterans must face
  no friction — re-entry is orientation, not re-teaching.
- Codex/bestiary and recap angles re-checked: both on the rejected/covered
  list (bestiary covered by Codex; "Last Delve recap" rejected). Boss
  entrance splash already exists (combat_screen _splash, 1600ms).

## Gap found

A player returning after days or weeks sees a title identical to the one
an everyday player sees. Nothing marks the absence — correctly, nothing
GUILTS it (§Ethics bans decaying streaks and loss-framed messaging) — but
nothing welcomes it either. The warm variant of this moment is entirely
missing, and the data says the moment decides whether the return sticks.

## Ship: v0.62.0 The Kept Fire

- `keptFireLine(MetaState, {now})` (title_screen.dart, injectable clock):
  when the NEWEST runHistory record is >= 7 calendar days old, one micro
  line under the Gathered Hearth (key `kept-fire-line`):
  "N days since your last delve — the hearth kept its fire."
- Pure derived state: no new persistence, no session flags. Banking any
  run makes the newest date today — the line retires itself.
- Silent for: fresh profiles, active players, malformed/absent dates.
- Calendar-day math (midnights crossed), so the wall-clock hour never
  changes the answer.

## Ethics check (§5)

The inverse of a decay mechanic: states that NOTHING was lost. No streak,
no count of missed days framed as cost, no ask, no push notification.
Endorse test: "if you've been away a week the title says how long and
that your fire is intact" — yes.
