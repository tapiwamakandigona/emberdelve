# v0.90.0 lead scout — The Counted Ration

## Field survey
v0.89.0 made the rest fire print its exact heal. The same honesty audit
flagged the shop: Field Rations say 'Heal 7 HP' unconditionally, but the
sim routes the purchase through `_heal`, which caps at max HP. At 28/30 a
25-gold ration heals 2 — the label promises 7. Slay the Spire's shop greys
and re-labels capped potions for exactly this reason. Mid-run screens
(shop 125 lines) remain the least-loved surface; this is the highest-value
one-line-of-truth fix on it.

## Decision
`healPreview(Sim, int amount)` — a pure no-mutation mirror of `_heal`'s cap
(never negative) in run_layer.dart beside restHealPreview, which refactors
onto it (one cap, one source of truth). Unsold ration rows print
'Heal 2 HP (28 to 30)' (NBSP-bound paren group, 'to' not an arrow — Cinzel
lesson from v0.89, and desc is Inter but wording stays consistent); at full
HP the row says 'Fully rested — heals nothing' so nobody wastes 25 gold.
SOLD rows keep the plain historic 'Heal 7 HP' label — a bought ration must
not relabel itself from the player's new HP.

## Rejected
- Disabling the buy button at full HP — behavior change; the honest label
  does the work without removing a (however unwise) choice.
- Shortfall text on unaffordable rows ('needs 20 more') — separate
  improvement, queued as its own release candidate.
- Counting dice/relic slots — their descriptions are already exact.
