# v0.84.0 lead scout — The Song Credit

## Finding

The music is the game's best-reviewed asset ("the music is 9/10", player
review 2026-08-23) — and the one asset the game never credits at the moment
it plays. Track names exist only in the Gramophone shelf and the summary's
grouped new-song line (v0.76.0), minutes after the hearing. The moment a
name can BIND to the music — the first time it starts — passes silently.
Standing practice elsewhere (Hades, Zelda) is the on-screen track credit.

## The improvement (one)

The first time a track ever plays, the existing flash toast names it:
'"Deeper Still" — first hearing'. Hooked exactly on `heardTracks.add(key)`
returning true, so it is once per track per profile, ever — at most eight
toasts in a profile's lifetime. Name resolves through the Gramophone
catalog (never credit a ghost key). The summary's grouped line stays: the
credit names the song while it plays; the summary banks the run's harvest.

## Rejected

- Persistent 'now playing' chip — standing chrome for a transient fact;
  the Gramophone is the standing surface.
- Crediting every hearing — noise; once-ever matches kept-fire/first-words
  quietness.
- ♪ glyph in the toast — font coverage risk for zero information.
