# v0.17.0 "The Even Scales" — data-driven character balance pass

Studio lesson (live balancing is continuous post-launch work at every
studio studied in docs/improvements/studio-priorities-2026-08-16.md):
balance from measured outcomes, not vibes. Our sealed sim + autoplay bot
make this fully reproducible.

## Method

`tool/balance_sweep_probe.dart`: every character × every difficulty ×
150 fixed seeds (1..150), same bot policy for all. The bot is a proxy —
absolute win rates differ from humans — but the *relative* spread across
characters under one policy is a fair tuning signal.

CVD note: a colorblind audit (store plates × Machado protan/deutan/tritan
matrices) ran first and PASSED — icons/labels/position carry all meaning,
color is decorative. No accessibility release needed; recorded in
progress.md 2026-08-16.

## Baseline (before)

| character | easy | normal | hard |
|-----------|------|--------|------|
| kindler   | 92.0 | 69.3   | 50.0 |
| warden    | 98.0 | 91.3   | **84.7** |
| gambler   | 91.3 | 56.0   | 34.7 |
| ascetic   | 87.3 | 50.7   | **24.0** |

Hard-mode spread: 60.7 points. The Warden on HARD was easier than the
Kindler on NORMAL — and hard is the paid unlock, so its difficulty
promise must hold.

## What was tried (iterations, each re-swept)

1. HP only (38→34 / 26→27 / 22→25): warden 84.7→83.3. HP is not the
   lever; the free mitigation engine is.
2. Warden relic iron_scale→kite_charm: **worse** (90.7) — per-block +1
   compounds with the ward die's block_bonus; the bot blocks often.
3. Warden keeps Iron Scale, but starts with `d4_guard` (Slate Chip,
   block-only d4 min-2) instead of `d6_ward` — the Slate Chip *forges
   into* the Ward Iron, so the old kit becomes an earned upgrade path:
   62.7 on hard.
4. Warden 34→32 HP: 56.0 on hard. Locked.

## Final (after)

| character | easy | normal | hard | change |
|-----------|------|--------|------|--------|
| kindler   | 92.0 | 69.3   | 50.0 | none (the anchor) |
| warden    | 93.3 | 80.0   | 56.0 | 38→32 HP, d6_ward→d4_guard start |
| gambler   | 92.0 | 60.0   | 37.3 | 26→27 HP |
| ascetic   | 92.7 | 62.7   | 30.7 | 22→26 HP |

Hard-mode spread: 25.3 points, ordered by intended risk profile
(tank > baseline > high-variance > glass cannon). Easy stays a
gentle on-ramp for everyone (92–93%).
