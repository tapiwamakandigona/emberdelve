# v0.124.0 lead scout — The Mender's Mark

Trail: gameplay content is the retention deep end, but the anchor
audit (v0.123.0 scout) closed the relic/event/enemy lanes: all
re-roll published promises. The temper system is the one combat
surface the golden bot never touches (v7 policy), so a new rune is
REAL mechanics at zero re-anchor — the cheapest gameplay/cost ratio
on the board.

Choices:
- Sustain over flash: the rune axes were damage/defense/economy/
  tempo; every roguelite's temper-equivalent list (StS's Lesson
  Learned line, Monster Train's unit upgrades) carries a heal
  option because HP is the resource players FEEL. 1 HP per trigger
  ≈ aegis-2-block in expected value on a d6 face — swept judgment,
  not a guess: triggers ~1/6 of rolls of one die.
- Either verb (unlike blade/aegis): a mend die is never a dead
  temper on the wrong action, which is the beginner-friendly slot
  — the rune you can't misuse.
- The 320 'overflow' was chased to the tool's missing fonts before
  touching product code — the rest screen was never broken. Verify
  the failure is REAL before fixing where it appears to be.
