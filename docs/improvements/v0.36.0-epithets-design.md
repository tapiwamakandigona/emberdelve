# v0.36.0 — The Epithets (design)

**One improvement:** earned **epithets** — titles the player wears under their
delver's name. `The Kindler, the Unburnt`. Chosen on the character screen,
carried onto the run summary's shareable Delver's Card.

## Why this, why now

- Third answer to the first outside review's "character customisation" ask:
  dyes (v0.27.0) recolor the body, vistas (v0.35.0) recolor the world,
  epithets name the *player's own history*. Identity/expression is hook #1 on
  the retention map, and titles are the cheapest strong form of it.
- Compounds v0.34.0's acquisition hook: a shared Delver's Card now carries an
  earned title, which is both social proof ("how do I get *the Unburnt*?")
  and a conversation starter — the LootShrine/ghost-challenge research shows
  shared artifacts work hardest when they encode standing, not just numbers.
- Zero new assets (text only — APK does not grow, per owner's size concern),
  zero sim changes (simVersion stays 7), all charter-clean: unlock lines
  state the real milestone, nothing is sold, nothing decays.

## Shape

- `lib/data/epithets.dart` — CONTENT AS DATA. `EpithetDef {id, title,
  unlockLine, stat, target, param?}` reusing the Ledger's exact stat
  vocabulary (`lib/data/achievements.dart`) so `statValue()` in
  `lib/meta/achievements.dart` resolves unlocks — one honesty contract, one
  resolver, no new counters. Unlocks are DERIVED, never persisted.
- 8 epithets, early → endgame: the Delver (win 1), the Thorough (10 runs),
  the Exact (25 exact kills), the Unresting (win w/o rest), the Bossbane
  (all 3 bosses), the Emberled (1,000 lifetime embers), the Unburnt (hard
  win), the Highborne (ascension 5). Plus the default: no epithet at all.
- `MetaState.selectedEpithet` (default `''` = none; compact toJson; fromJson
  validates against the map; cloud merge takes the fresher side, same as
  vistas/dyes).
- `GameController.epithetUnlocked(id)` re-checks the gate via `statValue`;
  `selectEpithet(id)` allows `''` (take the title off).
- Character screen: THE EPITHET section (vista-card pattern, ValueKey
  `epithet-$id`); the delver header shows the worn title.
- `DelverCardFacts.epithetTitle` → rendered after the delver name on the
  card and in the clipboard fallback text.

## Deliberately NOT included

- No epithets for sale, ever (spec §Ethics — expression is earned).
- No streak/decay epithets; nothing can be lost once earned.
- No new MetaState counters — only stats the Ledger already banks.
