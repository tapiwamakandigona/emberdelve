# v0.87.0 lead scout — The Guttering Foe

## Finding

The 30% rule now speaks three times — the danger music (v0.23.0), the
Narrow Climb (v0.85.0), the Foe's Last Thread (v0.86.0) — but never DURING
the fight, and never about the foe while the player can still act on it.
A player deciding whether to spend a surge die or bank block cannot see
that the foe is one push from falling; the enemy bar reads the same red at
90% and at 9%. The information exists, is honest, and is tactically the
most valuable fact in the room. (The rule is also duplicated in three
call sites — controller._inDanger and two summary helpers — inviting
drift.)

## The improvement (one)

The enemy HP bar gutters when the foe is inside the rule: fill turns gold
(the player's good-news colour) and the caption reads
'ENEMY HP · NEARLY SPENT · TURN N'. Screen readers get it free — StatBar's
semantics speak the label. One shared `inTheRed(hp, maxHp)` helper now
owns the rule; the controller's danger bed and both summary lines call it,
so the four surfaces can never disagree.

## Rejected

- A pulsing/flame animation on the bar — motion cost in the hot combat
  path, and reduce-motion players would lose the tell entirely; colour +
  text serves everyone.
- Showing exact "one more hit fells it" math — depends on assignment,
  relics, block; the game would sometimes be wrong. The rule states a
  fact, never a promise.
- Player-side mirror on the HP bar — the danger music + vignette already
  tell the player's side; doubling it is noise.
