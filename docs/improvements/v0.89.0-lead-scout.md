# v0.89.0 lead scout — The Counted Rest

## Where studios point
The industry framing (Schell's "interest curve", Supergiant/Slay-the-Spire
postmortems on decision quality) is consistent: at a fork, players should be
weighing OPTIONS, not doing arithmetic. Every point of hidden math is a point
of trust spent. Slay the Spire's rest site prints the literal heal amount on
the button; ours prints a percentage and makes the player derive it.

## The gap
`Rest — heal 30%` forces three derivations: 30% of my max, minus the
overheal cap, plus whatever my rest relics add. Bedroll (+4) and Hearth
Kettle (+6) are bought precisely for this moment and are INVISIBLE at it —
paid value the game never shows working.

## The pick
The rest button prints the exact outcome: `Rest — heal 9 HP (21 → 30)`.
- Numbers come from a new pure sim reader `restHealPreview(Sim)` that
  mirrors `runRest`+`_heal` exactly (base ⌊max·3/10⌋ + rest_bonus, capped) —
  one source of truth, the button can never lie.
- Overheal cap shown honestly (28/30 says heal 2, not 9).
- rest_bonus relics are naturally included, so Bedroll visibly pays rent.
- Full-HP branch ('Move on — fully rested') unchanged.

## Rejected
- Showing the % alongside the number — two encodings, one decision; noise.
- A breakdown line ('9 = 7 base + 2 Bedroll') — the fork needs the outcome,
  not the ledger; relic text already documents the bonus.
- Duplicating the formula in the UI layer — drift risk; the preview lives
  beside `runRest` in run_layer.dart instead.
- Also previewing forge/temper outcomes here — different scope, different
  release if ever.
