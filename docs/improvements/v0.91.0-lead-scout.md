# v0.91.0 lead scout — The Legible Stall

## Field survey
The v0.90.0 plates caught the shop slot's single-row layout squeezing title
and description into ~40% of the panel: at 320px titles broke MID-WORD
('FIELD RATION / S') and relic descriptions wrapped every two words
('the start of / every turn, and / blocks grant / +1.'). Names must never
break; a shop is read before it is bought from.

## Decision
Slot restructure: top row = lead · title · price button; description spans
the full panel width below. Title wrapped in FittedBox(scaleDown,
centerLeft) — a name scales down a step rather than ever breaking
(StatBar/player-name precedent, v0.87). Verified by plates at 320x568
(scaled one-line 'FIELD RATIONS', natural cap wording), 360x640, and
600x900 tablet clamp.

## Rejected
- Shrinking the 64px lead column — treats the symptom, keeps cramped descs.
- LayoutBuilder width switching — needless machinery, and the title-column
  IntrinsicHeight hazard.
- Moving the price under the desc — buttons at a row's right edge is the
  app-wide grammar; breaking it for one screen costs more than it buys.
