# v0.50.0 — The Tinker (sixth delver)

## Why this improvement

The roster's variance axis ran from the Gambler (widest swings) through the
Kindler (plain) with nothing at the narrow end. Control/consistency is a
proven archetype pull in roguelike deckbuilders (StS Defect turrets, Monster
Train Stygian control lists): a real share of players want to win by
eliminating downside, not by riding upside. Reviewer feedback (2026-08-23)
also asked for more character content.

pip ceiling (16 vs the Kindler's 18). "The delve is a machine; oil it."

## Re-anchor landmines found (and dodged)

1. **New relics resize the shop offer pool.** Shop offers draw
   `loot.range(1, relicPicks.length)` over all of `relicsOrder`
   (run_layer.dart ~L528). ANY new relic shifts every default-run offer draw
   → full golden re-anchor. So the Tinker reuses `loaded_pips` — the same
   kiln_key/whetstone precedent the Peddler and Ascetic set.
2. **The bot never spends relic `rerolls`.** `rerolls_left` has no reference
   in autoplay.dart, so a reroll-kit would sweep artificially weak, force
   overbuffing, and land overtuned for humans. min_roll is applied inside
   the roll helper — fully bot-legible with zero policy changes.

Net: **zero sim changes, zero golden movement** — a data-only character.
