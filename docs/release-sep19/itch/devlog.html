The Whole Company — Emberdelve 0.184.0

The whole company moves. All twenty-two delvers have refreshed designs, articulated arms and boots, and tools held in their moving hands. A cut, a crush, a thrust, a stamp, a hook and a pick keep their own motions.

Health, wounds and guard follow the moment of impact. This is a change to what you see, not to how the dice calculate damage. Tempered dice also show the right strength of swing for their true size.

There is now a saved Blood effects switch in Settings. Turn it off to hide blood, ichor, floor stains and wound marks; damage, guard and fatigue cues stay readable. Your dice, saves, delvers and Forge unlock stay as they were.
