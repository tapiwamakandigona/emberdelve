Emberdelve

An offline-first, turn-based dice-builder roguelite with visible enemy intent and fair deterministic resolution. Roll your pool, assign dice to attack and block, and forge a build as you descend. The enemy shows its next move. The same seed and choices produce the same delve.

0.184.0 — The Whole Company

All 22 playable delvers have refreshed designs and articulated combat bodies. Shoulders, elbows, wrists and boots move through each strike, with the signature tool held in the moving hand. Health, wounds and guard now follow the impact rather than changing ahead of it.

  • Your comfort, your choice. A saved Blood effects switch hides blood, ichor, floor stains and wound marks. Damage, guard and fatigue stay visible.
  • The right weight of swing. Tempered dice use their true size for visual strike strength. Rolls and damage are unchanged.
  • Keep what you earned. This release preserves your delvers, kits, dice, saves and Forge unlock.

Inside the delve

  • Build your pool. Draft, forge and upgrade dice. Split each roll between attack and block; combinations can make a small die matter.
  • Shape your run. Choose a keystone after the first won fight, or decline. Temper a face at a rest and give one die a lasting mark.
  • Read the danger. Visible enemy intent and branching paths let you weigh the fight against its reward before committing.
  • Return to the hearth. Bank embers, unlock delvers and dice, and start again. The Ledger and Codex keep the record; daily and weekly seeded delves offer another way down.

Fair by design

  • No ads, energy timers, decaying streaks or FOMO mechanics.
  • Play offline without an account. Optional analytics only runs if you enable it.
  • Full Easy and Normal runs, all delvers, daily and weekly delves remain free.
  • One optional Ember Forge purchase opens Hard and Ascension. No subscriptions or consumables.
  • Portrait controls, reduced-motion support and the saved Blood effects preference.
