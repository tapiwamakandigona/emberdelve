# v0.23.0 — The Deep Hum

One improvement: the delve map now has a living soundscape that deepens as
you descend.

## What changed

- The ember-crackle ambience bed (previously title/rest screens only) now
  runs under the map music, at a level tied to your descent: 12% of music
  volume on the first layer, rising linearly to 45% at the boss layer.
- Stepping a node deeper adjusts the running loop's volume live — it never
  restarts, so there is no hiccup or seam.
- Your music volume slider governs it exactly as before, and music muted
  keeps the bed silent.

## Why

The map is where a run breathes between fights, but until now layer 1 and
layer 9 sounded identical — the descent was visual only. The genre's best
atmosphere ties sound to progress; this does it with zero cost.

## Integrity

- Zero new assets: the bed reuses the shipped ember loop, so the download
  size is byte-for-byte unchanged from v0.22.0's engine payload.
- No new permissions, no network calls, no monetization changes.
- Determinism proof: the sealed sim is untouched — all 8 per-boss golden
  anchors are byte-identical to v0.22.0.
- New tests pin the level curve (endpoints, monotonicity, clamping), that
  the map bed stays under the title bed until the deep third, and that
  descent depth is forward-only across a full won run.
- Signed with the same certificate as every release
  (pin `031acb42566a51d5b59ffd5deb173f1b0e817a9edff1bb6979f68564d44b7a0d`).
