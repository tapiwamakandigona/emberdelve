# v0.70.0 — The Pictured Card

One improvement: **your delver now stands on the Delver's Card.** The card
is the only artifact that leaves the game — and until now it carried a
generic trophy or flame where the delver should be. v0.69.0 put the delver
on their own summary; this release puts them on the card you share.

## What changed

- **The delver's sprite replaces the trophy/flame** at the top of the card,
  painted in the dye they wear. The name line below already carried their
  worn title — now the portrait matches. A shared card is unmistakably
  *someone's* run, not a template.
- **Cards cut from the Ledger** paint the delver as they stand today —
  portraiture, not history (a coat is paint; the epitaph's worn-at-the-time
  title remains the narrative record).
- **Loss cards keep full color.** The export is a poster; a dimmed sprite
  in a group chat reads as a rendering bug, not dignity.

## Under the hood

- `DelverCardFacts` gains `charId` and `dyeId` — the card stays a pure
  function of its facts, so tests pin the portrait like every other line.
  Live cards resolve `meta.dyeFor`; Ledger cards pass meta explicitly.
- Sprite drawn static (no bob) with nearest-neighbor scaling — pixel-crisp
  at the 3x PNG export.
- New key: `card-delver`. Zero sim movement, zero save-format movement.
  Suite green, analyze clean (fatal-infos).

## Ethics

The card states what happened and who did it. Nothing is sold, teased, or
gated — the portrait uses only what the player already owns and wears.
