# v0.10.0 — The First Delve (build 36)

One improvement, shipped on its own (GitHub-only release; no Play submission
per owner directive 2026-08-16): **the tutorial wall is dead — the game now
teaches itself as you play.**

## For players

New delvers used to meet four cards of rules before rolling a single die.
Now your first delve teaches each rule at the exact moment it matters:

- **Your first fight** opens with one card only: roll, tap a die, spend it.
- **The first time the enemy acts**, a card points up at the intent badge —
  that move was announced, and it resolved exactly as shown. That's the
  house promise: the dark fights fair.
- **The first time your dice match**, a card names the pay: pairs add,
  triples ignite, straights earn a free risky reroll.
- **The first time a big hit is telegraphed**, a card says now is the turn
  to stack block — and why it melts by your next roll.

Each card shows once, ever. Tap anywhere to dismiss — nothing is forced,
nothing loops, and veterans who already finished the old tutorial never see
any of them. The ? button in a fight still replays the full how-to-play
deck any time.

## Under the hood

- New pure module `lib/game/tips.dart`: a `TipDirector` fed by the same sim
  event stream the UI already consumes. One tip at a time; a trigger that
  fires while another card is up simply recurs at its next natural moment.
  The sim itself is untouched — zero snapshot changes, all golden hashes
  unchanged.
- Seen-tips persist in the profile (`tipsSeen`), merge as a set union in
  cloud saves, and migrate: a save that finished the old wall pre-seeds all
  four. Dismissing the last tip also sets the legacy flag so older builds
  sharing a cloud save never replay their wall.
- Tip cards pass the 320×568 @1.3x text overflow sweep (the full-size
  anchor padding yields on short screens; a scroll wrapper is the
  can't-overflow guarantee) — enforced by a new test, not by eye.
- 15 new tests (director rules, persistence, migration, cloud merge, widget
  smoke: the wall no longer auto-runs, the staged card does). Full suite:
  372/372.

## Why this, first

Backlog `docs/improvements/studio-priorities-2026-08-16.md` §3: first-minute
feel. Front-loaded tutorial walls are the most-cited first-session drop-off
in mobile roguelites; contextual teaching is the pattern the genre's best
onboarding (and our own testers' behavior) points at. Deliberately NOT
shipped: "quick-win balance tuning" — that would touch the sealed sim, and
the easy on-ramp already covers it.
