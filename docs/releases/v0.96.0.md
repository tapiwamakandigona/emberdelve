# v0.96.0 — The Hearth Tale

Our first outside review finished easy mode and still said "I don't
understand what's a delve" — with the one-shot whats_a_delve tip live
since v0.30.0. One card at one moment is not how a world sinks in. Now
the world arrives as a drip: every rest fire tells one short tale.

- Ten hearth tales, told in a fixed order across the player's whole
  lifetime (persisted, cloud-max merged), then the arc comes round
  again. They answer, one fire at a time: what a delve is, what embers
  are, who the six delvers are, why relics are found, what wears a
  crown far below.
- The tale sits quoted under the hollow's subtitle — italic, dim,
  ambient. No card, no dismiss button, no interruption: lore you sit
  with, not lore you swipe away.
- The arc advances exactly when a hollow is left (rest, forge, or
  temper all count) — never on an invalid tap, never twice for one
  visit however the run is saved and resumed.

## Technical

- New lib/data/tales.dart: fixed `hearthTales` list + pure
  `hearthTale(int heard)` indexer (sequential first pass, then cycles;
  negative-safe against corrupt saves). New MetaState.hearthTalesHeard,
  monotonic, MAX on cloud merge; fresh saves omit the key so their
  bytes stay pre-0.96.0-identical.
- Advance rule lives in GameController.apply(): phase left 'rest' —
  the single mutation path, so no UI code can double-count.
- test/hearth_tale_test.dart pins the indexer, the ethics-blacklist +
  length charter over all tale copy, the advance rule (invalid command
  does not advance), the cloud merge, the save round-trip, and the
  rendered tale on the rest screen.
- Plate-critiqued at 360x640 and 320x568 (longest tale forced at the
  narrowest width; tool/hearth_tale_visual_test.dart).
