# v0.59.0 — The Proven

One improvement: **a tenth epithet crowns the Provings arc** — clear
every proving and "the Proven" is yours to wear.

## What changed

- New epithet `the_proven`, unlock "Clear every proving." (stat
  `provings_cleared`, target 10 — pinned by test to always equal the live
  provings count, so a future proving bumps the bar instead of orphaning
  the promise).
- `provings_cleared` joined the honest stat resolver
  (`meta.provingsCleared` Set size — junk-proof).
- The epithet flows everywhere titles already go: the delver header, the
  summary, the Delver's Card, and — since the fuller record (v0.57.0) —
  the Ledger's remembered rows.
- The Provings arc now rewards both its floor (Duskquartz vista at 3
  clears, v0.55.0) and its summit.

## Why

Completing all ten provings changed nothing but a counter. Identity is
the earned reward this game gives, and a finished gauntlet had none.

## Ethics

- Earned by delving, never sold — like every epithet.
- Zero sim changes; goldens and pinned sweeps untouched.
