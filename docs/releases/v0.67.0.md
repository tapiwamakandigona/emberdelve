# v0.67.0 — The Dyed Delver

One improvement: **dyes are now worn per delver.** The last wardrobe choice
that still repainted everyone at once now belongs to each delver alone — buy
Emberwash once and put it on the Kindler while the Warden keeps Frostveil.
Direct follow-through on the first outside player ask ("You could add
character customisation") and the exact sibling of v0.66.0's per-delver
epithets.

## What changed

- **Each delver keeps their own coat.** The combat stage, the map marker, the
  delver picker, the dye swatches, and the title hearth all paint the dye the
  *specific* delver wears. The hearth is the payoff: your delvers gather
  around the fire, each in the color you chose for them.
- **The wardrobe gains the name-chip row** (the same pills as the epithet
  shelf) once a second delver joins. Both sections dress the same selected
  delver — one delver is "being dressed" on the screen at a time. The dye
  swatch now shows that delver wearing the candidate color.
- **Ownership stays global.** Embers buy a dye once; every delver may wear
  it. No per-delver re-purchase, no price multiplication — real prices up
  front, as always.
- **Old choices are honored.** A pre-v0.67.0 dye keeps painting every delver
  until you dress one differently (the legacy selection survives as a
  fallback and is never written again). Old saves are untouched.

## Under the hood

- `MetaState.charDye` (delver id → dye id) + `dyeFor(charId)` resolver with
  legacy `activeDye` fallback; unknown delver/dye ids dropped on decode;
  empty map emits no JSON key (fresh saves byte-identical).
- `setActiveDye(id, {required forChar})` guards ownership and unlocked
  delvers; cloud merge takes the fresher side's coats wholesale (same
  convention as `charEpithet`).
- Zero sim movement, zero save-format breakage. Suite green, analyze clean
  (fatal-infos).

## Ethics

Pure cosmetics with real prices shown up front — a dye never changes stats,
dice, or hit-boxes. No timers, no FOMO, no per-delver squeeze. Earned embers
buy it once, forever, for everyone at the hearth.
