# v0.118.0 — The Flintwright

A seventh delver — the SWARM archetype: the only delver who starts
with FOUR dice, and none of them grand.

- **The Flintwright** — 24 HP, no relic, and a pocketful of shards:
  Flint Shard ×2, Spark Chip, Slate Chip. More rolls, smaller
  promises, twice the forge futures. 750 embers at the hearth.
- Balance swept 400 seeds per difficulty before shipping. First cut
  (28 HP) came in too strong (90.5/65.5/41.25%); 24 HP landed
  87.5/57.25/34.25 — inside the roster band (tinker: 85.25/58.25/
  31.0). HP was the knob; the four-die kit is the identity.
- Signature weapon: the Knapping Pick — short reach, the tool that
  makes shards; the flintwright fights close, like their dice.
- Sprite: palette variant of the warden sheet (HSV remap, the
  documented v0.50.0 tinker method; warden as source so the
  silhouette differs from the tinker). Recorded in PROVENANCE.md.
- The seventh chair at the fire: a Named Company codex story ships
  with them.
- Pure data — existing dice only, no new relic — so seeded runs for
  every other delver are byte-identical. NO golden re-anchor.
- UI fix the roster forced: 'The Flintwright' is the first name long
  enough to break mid-word at 320 dp — locked-card titles now fit
  via FittedBox (the given-name rule, applied to roster names).
