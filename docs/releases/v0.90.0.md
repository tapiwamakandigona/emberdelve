# v0.90.0 — The Counted Ration

v0.89.0 made the rest fire print its exact heal. This release carries the
same honesty to the shop, where it costs real gold: Field Rations said
'Heal 7 HP' unconditionally, but the sim caps healing at max HP — at 28/30
that 25-gold purchase heals 2. The label now counts:

- `Heal 7 HP (17 to 24)` — the real heal and where it lands.
- At full health: 'Fully rested — heals nothing'. The buy stays possible —
  we don't remove choices — but nobody spends 25 gold uninformed.
- SOLD rows keep the plain historic label; a bought ration must not
  relabel itself from the player's new HP.

## Honesty rules

- One cap, one source of truth: new pure reader `healPreview(Sim, amount)`
  in the sim mirrors `_heal`'s overheal arithmetic exactly, and v0.89's
  `restHealPreview` now refactors onto it — the two counted surfaces can
  never disagree. Parity tests prove the promise equals the heal the buy
  then performs, including on a NATURAL overheal cap (pinned seed 20:
  31/34 HP, ration 8 → heals 3).

## Technical

- `healPreview` in sim/run_layer.dart; shop_screen prints it. NBSPs bind
  '(17 to 24)' so narrow screens break before the parenthesis, never
  inside it (v0.89 lesson). Tests +5 (`test/ration_preview_test.dart`;
  new pinned shop seeds: kindler easy 7 uncapped 17/30 gold 98, 20 natural
  cap, 6 full-HP). Plate-critiqued at 360x640 and 320x568. Zero sim
  movement — read-only function addition; goldens untouched. A
  pre-existing narrow-width slot-title wrap defect found on plates is
  documented and queued for v0.91.0.
