with two *different* smith's marks — a Blade sharpened into the Deep
Coal's 8, an Aegis worked into an Ember Die's 6. One face cuts, one
face holds.

- Unlocks at 1650 embers; the ladder keeps ascending.

  vs kindler 89.75/67.25/41.50 — in band at the first HP guess.
