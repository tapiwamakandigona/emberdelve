# v0.49.0 — The Shorter Road

A new way to delve: the **Short Delve** — six floors instead of nine, a whole
delve sized for a shorter sit.

## The Short Road

- **Six floors, the full grammar.** Elite, hearth, shop, boss — everything a
  delve is, in roughly half the time. Flip the **Short Delve** toggle under
  the title-screen difficulty selector; it composes with any difficulty and
  ascension, and it remembers your choice.
- **Rewards run deeper to fit the climb.** Dice offers and shop stock open
  their tiers three floors early, and purses run heavier (fight gold ×1.5),
  so a short build still walks the full tier-1 → tier-3 arc before the door.
  Ember payouts are untouched — fewer fights simply pay fewer embers.
- **The heaviest foes are tuned to match.** Elites and the boss stand floors
  earlier than the long delve tuned them for, so they fight shaved (elites
  −2 / ×0.80 HP, boss −3 / ×0.58 HP). Regular floors are exactly the long
  delve's first six. All knobs were tuned by 400-seed sweeps until the same
  fairness bands held on the short format as on the long one.
- **Shareable, exactly.** Delve Codes now carry the format — a code cut from
  a short run reproduces the same six-layer delve, seed and all. Codes from
  every earlier version still work unchanged.
- **Short Day.** The Trials calendar gains a mutator day where the shared
  daily delve runs the Short Road; the Weekly rotation can now deal a Short
  Week too.

Classic nine-layer runs are byte-identical to v0.48.0 — the sim goldens and
every pinned seed prove it.

## For players

Nothing to migrate. If a delve has ever felt one bus stop too long, the
toggle is waiting under the difficulty selector.
