# v0.133.0 — The Six Marks

The temper collection arc, mirroring the Weekly's (v0.127): with
two marks a delve (v0.132) and six runes on the anvil, WHICH runes
you have worked becomes a history worth keeping.

- **meta.runesTempered** — every rune ever tempered, banked at run
  end win or lose (forge work counts, the tempersSet rule). A die
  later lost still counts: the work happened.
- **The Third Mark** (3 distinct) and **The Six Marks** (every
  rune the anvil offers — promise-worded, target pinned to the
  live rune set). 64 achievements.
- Junk-proof twice: only faceRunes ids enter the bank at write,
  and the resolver counts only live ids at read (the v0.127 idiom
  at both ends).
