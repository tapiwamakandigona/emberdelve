# v0.22.0 — The Crowned Deep

Two new bosses hold the deep. This is the second return-moment content drop
(after v0.12.0 "New Embers"), aimed at the one lever that hadn't moved since
v0.5.0: the run's climax.

## New

- **Slag Regent** (boss) — *the drawbridge*: two guard beats in perfect
  silence, then a band-max swing. Count, bank your burst, spend it on beat
  three.
- **Hearthless King** (boss) — *the pendulum*: the only strict 2-beat boss.
  Every other beat is free; the hits are the largest regular-cadence swings
  in the game.
- **Ashglass Sentinel** and **Coal-Seam Wyrm** (late-band regulars) — each
  rehearses one king's rhythm at a survivable scale, so the deep teaches you
  before it tests you.
- **Regent's Causeway** and **The King's Toll** (events).
- **Siege Hook** (+1 attacks, +2 more vs elites/bosses) and **King's
  Ransom** (+6 gold, +2 embers per won fight) (relics).
- 8 new Codex entries.

## Fairness, measured

- The boss rota is a pure function of the run seed, so the daily trial can
  now crown a king — everyone faces the same boss that day.
- Per-boss autoplay win rates stay inside the roster's spread on both
  difficulties (normal: incumbents 60–77%, Regent 68%, King 60%; hard:
  incumbents 30–59%, Regent 51%, King 29%). Both newcomers were tuned to
  these measurements before shipping — first cuts were harder and were
  softened, not the other way round.
- 1000-seed difficulty bands vs v0.21.0: easy and normal unchanged, hard
  43.2% vs 45.3% — within sampling noise of the pool remap, with the
  residual being two deliberately climactic kings.
- Golden determinism anchors re-measured and re-pinned for the 8-boss
  roster (the third deliberate re-anchor), with a stronger scheme: every
  per-boss anchor seed is now *proven* to reach its boss.
