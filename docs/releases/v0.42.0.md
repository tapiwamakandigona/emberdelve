# v0.42.0 — The Gathered Hearth

The title screen's campfire is no longer a lonely place. Every delver you
have unlocked now idles at the hearth — the kindler in their old spot beside
the flames, and each delver you've earned seated alternately around the fire,
facing it. Your roster is visible the moment the game opens: collection
progress as a scene, not a counter.

## What changed

- **The Gathered Hearth** — the title fire seats every unlocked delver in
  unlock-price order. Left side faces right, right side faces the flames.
  A fresh profile renders the exact scene it always did: one kindler, one
  fire, pixel for pixel.
- A fuller hearth seats slightly smaller delvers (72px alone or in a pair,
  64px for three, 58px for four or five) so a full company still reads as
  one campfire, and the whole gathering scales down before it can ever
  overflow a 320px screen.
- Your active dye applies to the whole company — the wardrobe is yours, so
  everyone at your fire wears it.

## Why

Player-facing progress that asks for nothing back. Unlocking a delver
already pays in gameplay; now it also pays every single boot, quietly.
No badge, no counter, no prompt to unlock more — the empty seats simply
aren't shown (§Ethics: the scene states what you have, never what you lack).
