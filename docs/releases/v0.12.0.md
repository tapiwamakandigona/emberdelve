# v0.12.0 — New Embers (build 38)

One improvement, shipped on its own (GitHub-only release; no Play submission
per owner directive 2026-08-16): **the delve got deeper — five new enemies,
three new rooms, four new relics.**

## For players

- **Five new foes**, each with a rhythm the roster didn't have yet — and two
  of them are quiet previews of boss patterns, learnable while the stakes
  are low:
  - **Tinder Mote** (early) — the metronome. The exact same hit, every beat,
    forever. The only perfectly even enemy in the game.
  - **Slag Snail** (early) — guards and swings on the same beat, every beat.
    The Bellows' pressure, at a pace you can survive learning.
  - **Vent Serpent** (deep) — a five-beat cycle, the longest of any regular.
    The Cinder Hierophant's long count, before you ever meet him.
  - **Pumice Hulk** (deep) — two heavy hits back to back, then a guard.
    Punishes hoarding block for one big swing.
  - **Cinder Marshal** (elite) — two guarded swings, then an unguarded
    finisher. The whole fight is about holding your burst for beat three.
- **Three new rooms**: the Serpent's Molt, the Snail Road, and the Marshal's
  Muster — the event deck grows 28 → 31, so an evening of runs repeats
  itself even less.
- **Four new relics**: the Cinder Lantern (the game's first twin-blessing
  relic — steady block *and* stronger blocks), the Serpent Fang, the Pumice
  Plate, and the Hearth Kettle.
- Every new enemy lands in the Codex with lore to unseal and a ledger record
  to fill in. Five new `Not yet met.` lines are waiting.

## Fairness, measured (not promised)

- Balance sweep after the drop: easy ~92%, normal ~70%, hard ~47% autoplay
  win rate over the seed sweep; the difficulty curve stays strictly
  monotonic across every keystone/temper combination and the full ascension
  ladder (0 → 3 → 6 → 12 → 20).
- 24,000-command fuzz across all phases: zero invariant breaks.
- No new mechanics, no new economy, and nothing moved behind the paywall:
  the new commons and their records are free content on every difficulty.

## Under the hood

- Pure content-as-data: `enemies.dart` 30 → 35, `events.dart` 28 → 31,
  `relics.dart` 22 → 26, plus nine Codex entries. Zero sim-logic changes.
- New ids appended at the end of their spawn bands; boss count deliberately
  stays at six so the golden-anchor seed still maps to the Ember Tyrant.
- Pool growth reshuffles what seeded runs draw, so all seven golden replay
  anchors were re-measured from real runs (twice each, identical) and
  re-pinned — the old → new record is in `progress.md`.
- First multi-hook relic (Cinder Lantern) — safe by construction, since
  relic hooks are summed per-key across owned relics.

## Why this, now

Backlog `docs/improvements/studio-priorities-2026-08-16.md`: after v0.11's
Ledger made every unmet enemy a visible goal, a bestiary drop is the
cheapest honest return-moment — new `Not yet met.` lines plug straight into
the loop the Ledger already built.
