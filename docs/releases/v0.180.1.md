**Status: RELEASED 2026-09-05 — https://github.com/tapiwamakandigona/emberdelve/releases/tag/v0.180.1**

## More from Tsoro Studios (Settings)

Settings gains a single "MORE FROM TSORO STUDIOS" section directly below the
unlock-code Redeem row: one line per sibling game (name, one-line hook, a
dense button). Today that is **Fliptide** — "One tap flips gravity. Same
course for everyone today." — which opens https://tsorostudios.itch.io/fliptide
until its Play listing is public (`kFliptideOnPlay`); Pyregrove's entry stays
behind `kShowPyregrove = false` until its listing is public.

Android tries `market://details?id=…&referrer=utm_source%3Demberdelve` first
and falls back to https; web builds open the https URL in a new tab. No badge,
no modal, never auto-opens, no telemetry event (the Play install-referrer is
the measurement). New dependency: `url_launcher` — nothing else changes; no
ads, no tracking, no new permissions.

## Tests

## Campfire honours Reduce motion (PR #100)

With **Reduce motion** on, the title campfire keeps its glow but stops
animating (and stops its ticker). The painter now also repaints when the
palette changes, so cosmetic swaps apply without leaving the screen.
`test/campfire_motion_test.dart` pins both behaviours.
