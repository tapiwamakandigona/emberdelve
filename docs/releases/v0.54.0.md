# Emberdelve v0.54.0 — The Epitaph

The Delver's Card now tells your story, not just your score.

## The Epitaph

- The shareable card (win or loss) carries the run's epitaph under your
  name — *"Here fell The Kindler — Coal-Seam Wyrm ended the run on
  floor 4."* — the same honest voice as the summary screen's Obituary,
  cut to card size.
- Wins name the boss you felled; losses name what ended you and where.
  Nothing invented: every name and floor is a banked fact from the run.
- The plain-text fallback (when sharing isn't available) carries the
  story too.

## Notes

- Zero gameplay changes — same sim, same seeds, same odds.
- Same honesty rules as ever: losses are dignified, the card never asks
  anyone to spend, and sharing stays player-initiated.

Thank you for playing — and for every card that lands in a group chat.
