# Emberdelve v0.52.0 — "The Tinker's Proving"

One improvement: the tenth proving fills the last empty seat, and a new
epithet stands beside it.

## The Tinker's Proving

- **The Provings gain a tenth entry.** *The Tinker's Proving* — the Tinker
  on normal floors, seed 131. Like every proving before it, the seed is
  machine-proven winnable by the autoplay bot before it shipped, and the bot
  plays worse than you do. Steady pips under Loaded Pips: the plan, not the
  roll, decides this delve.
- **Every delver now has a proving.** The roster coverage that started with
  the Ninth Proving (v0.41.0) is whole again after the Tinker joined in
  v0.50.0: kindler ×2, warden ×2, gambler ×2, ascetic ×2, peddler ×1,
  tinker ×1.
- **A new epithet: the Well-Oiled.** Worn for winning a delve as the Tinker.
  It reads the same real, banked counter as the Ledger's *Well Oiled* entry —
  derived, never persisted, never sold, honest to the milestone it states.

## Honesty notes

- Data-only release: zero simulation changes. Golden hashes, difficulty
  sweeps, and every pinned seed are untouched by construction.
- Clearing the proving pays nothing and unlocks nothing — the clear mark is
  the whole prize, same stance as every proving (§Ethics).
