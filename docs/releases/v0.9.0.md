# v0.9.0 — Today's Trials (build 35)

One improvement, shipped on its own (GitHub-only release; no Play submission
per owner directive 2026-08-16): **the Daily Delve's rule now rotates**.

## For players

Every calendar date now declares ONE trial on top of the Daily Delve's
shared seed — same trial for every player, everywhere, no server involved:

- **Mutator days.** Some days the daily plays under one declared modifier —
  Flint Day (every die rolls as a d4), Elite Day (every regular fight is an
  elite), or Lean Day (no shops). The rule is spelled out under the Daily
  Delve button before you commit, exactly like the weekly's.
- **Goal days.** Other days the rules are vanilla but the date declares a
  bonus objective — Full Purse (end with 40+ gold), Warpath (win 4+ fights),
  Light Step (3+ floors untouched), or Ember Hoard (gather 60+ embers in
  the delve). Meet it and a small ember bonus banks with your run; the
  summary says "Trial met ✦ +N embers". Miss it and nothing happens —
  no penalty, no nag, not even a message.
- **Shared results carry the rule.** The daily share text's header now
  names the trial ("Emberdelve Daily 2026-08-16 · Flint Day"), so a copied
  result also says what rule it was played under.

Same charter as ever (§Ethics): no streaks, no expiry, no pressure copy.
A missed day is simply a missed day.

## Under the hood

- New pure modules: `lib/game/trials.dart` (date → trial via a namespaced
  domain hash, `emberdelve-trial:YYYY-MM-DD`; goal judge as a pure function
  over the final run snapshot + the v0.8.0 RunTrace) and the catalog-as-data
  `lib/data/trials.dart` (7 entries, append-only `trialsOrder`).
- The sim is untouched — simVersion stays 7, every golden hash unchanged.
  Mutator days ride the existing `cmd['mutators']` seam with ids the sim
  already knows from the Weekly Delve; goal days are pure observation.
- Nothing new persists: the trial is re-derived from the daily's saved date
  label, so resumed dailies keep their trial for free and old saves are
  untouched. The bonus banks once, through the same guarded path as every
  other end-of-run credit.
- Forward compatible: an unknown goal id evaluates as "no goal", so a
  future catalog entry can never crash an old build.

## Verification

- `flutter analyze` clean (fatal-on-warnings CI gate; also silenced two

- Full suite green, including 15 new tests: catalog integrity (every trial
