# v0.72.0 — The Given Name

One improvement: **your delvers are yours to name.** A player asked for
character customisation; the identity arc already let each delver keep
their own best floor, worn title, and worn dye — but they all still
answered to the roster's names. Now you tap a delver's name on the picker
and give them your own. It follows them everywhere their story is told:
the run summary, the ledger, the obituary and epitaph, and the Delver's
Card you share.

## What changed

- **Tap-to-name on the roster.** Every unlocked delver's name is now an
  edit affordance (quiet pencil, no button chrome). A small dialog asks
  once — *"Theirs by birth: The Kindler. Leave the field empty to give it
  back."* — 16 characters, free, always reversible.
- **The name travels.** Summary header, ledger history rows and the
  delver roster in the ledger's stats page, share cards,
  and banked obituaries/epitaphs all speak the given name. Locked delvers
  keep their roster names untouchable.
- **Fits, never clips.** A full 16-character name scales down a touch on
  narrow screens instead of losing its tail to an ellipsis.
- **Cloud-safe.** Names ride the save like worn titles and dyes: the
  fresher profile's names win wholesale on merge; unknown ids and junk
  characters are dropped on load.

## Under the hood

- `MetaState.charName` map + `nameFor(charId)` resolver (falls back to
  the roster name); `sanitizeGivenName` strips control characters,
  collapses whitespace, clamps to 16.
- `GameController.setDelverName` — unlocked-only, empty restores the true
  name, no-op guard, persisted immediately.
- Zero sim movement, zero pinned-seed movement. Suite green (+6 tests),
  analyze clean (fatal-infos).

## Ethics

Naming is free for every unlocked delver — no ember gate, no purchase,
nothing expires. No filter pretends to moralize your words: it's your
device and your delver; we only strip characters that would break the
save or the card.
