# v0.48.0 — The Iron Between

**One improvement:** elite fights now carry their own battle theme — **"Iron Between"** (Kevin MacLeod, "Volatile Reaction", CC BY 4.0) — a harder drum between the common fights and the crowned deep, and the eighth collectible record in the Gramophone.

## What's new

- **A theme for the iron.** Until now, elite foes borrowed the crowned-boss music. The ladder is finally audible: regular fights play *Steel and Ember*, elites play *Iron Between*, and only a crowned foe raises *The Deep Crowned*. Your ears know the stakes before the first die lands — and the boss theme means "boss" again.
- **An eighth Gramophone record.** *Iron Between* unlocks the honest way — cross blades with an elite — with the plain hint "Cross blades with an elite foe." Once heard, it replays at the hearth like every other record.
- **Nothing else moves.** Backgrounds, combat rules, enemies and balance are untouched; existing profiles keep every record they have already earned.
- **Sourced and processed like its siblings**: same Kevin MacLeod family as the combat and boss themes, gained to −19 LUFS (true peak −9.2 dBFS), edge-faded, OGG q4 stereo — full recipe in `PROVENANCE.md`, attribution added to Credits.

## Why this hook

Players told us the music carries the game ("the music is 9/10") and that the boss designs land. Spending that credit on *stakes you can hear* is the cheapest honest tension in the genre — Slay the Spire, Hades and Darkest Dungeon all mark their elite/miniboss encounters in the score, because a theme shift is felt as danger without a single UI element changing. It also repairs a real dilution: when elites and bosses share one piece, the crowned moment is spent early. Now the iron between you and the crown has its own sound, and first hearing it is a collectible moment rather than a reused cue.

## Deliberately NOT included

- No purchased tracks, no timed unlocks — the record is earned by fighting, full stop (spec §Ethics).
- No per-elite themes: one piece keeps the signal clean and the download small (2.4 MB, in family with its siblings).
- No sim changes: `lib/sim/` sealed, simVersion 7; goldens and fairness bands untouched.
