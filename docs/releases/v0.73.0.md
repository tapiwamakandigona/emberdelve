# v0.73.0 — The Opened Vista

One improvement: **the delve now tells you when a new vista opens.** Earned
titles got their announcement in v0.68.0; the other earned cosmetic — the
vistas a reviewer called "backgrounds" — unlocked in total silence. Fell
your fifteenth distinct foe and the verdigris stood open without a word.
Now the run summary says so, once, in the same breath as the result:

> The Moonveil vista stands open.

## What changed

- **One quiet gold line per vista** a run's banking opens, alongside the
  earned-name and deepest-mark lines. Win or loss alike — a loss on the
  ninth floor earns the Deepshale, and is told so.
- **Never re-announced, never retroactive.** The diff is taken inside a
  single bank pass (open before vs open after), so veterans' already-open
  vistas stay silent and nothing can double-fire.
- **No persistence, no migration.** Vista unlocks remain pure derived
  state; the announcement is transient like a rank-up.

## Under the hood

- `pendingVistas` on the controller, same shape as `pendingEpithets`;
  summary keys `opened-vista-<id>`.
- Zero sim movement, zero save-format movement. Suite green (+4 tests),
  analyze clean (fatal-infos).

## Ethics

States a fact about colors already earned. No "N more marks to the next
vista" teaser — where to wear them is the delver screen's business.
