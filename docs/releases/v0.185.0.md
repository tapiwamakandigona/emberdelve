# 0.185.0 — Living Foes

## For players

- **Foes have bodies.** Wisps, moths and shades hover off their shadow.
  Brutes, golems and bosses heave, and crawlers scuttle. When a foe attacks,
  it dashes to a spot right beside your hero, running with its own run
  animation where its sprite sheet has one. Brutes hop in and land with a
  thump, crawlers pounce, wisps dart in flat and maws and golems lunge low. The time from the start of an attack to the
  hit is still 440 ms, and the wind-up is easier to read.
- **Getting hit reads clearly.** Your hero no longer freezes as a white
  cut-out. There is one short flash, then a small stumble back and a red glow
  for about 120 ms.
- **Kills read clearly.** A slain foe crumbles into embers of its own art,
  and the heat sweeps in from the side the blow came from. An exactly lethal
  blow gets its own ember-white ring. The damage number holds beside the foe,
  and the "+EMBERS — EXACT!" or "OVERKILL" call-out has its own slot. The
  foe's HP bar drains to empty.
- **The final boss.** Its kill no longer turns the whole screen white. A warm
  glow stays inside the stage and a VICTORY! banner rises with embers while
  your hero lifts their weapon. Once a fight is decided, the dice and End turn
  go quiet. The last kill no longer points you to a next foe that isn't there.
- **Clearer screens.** Small messages such as "Healed 4" now show as a pill
  near the top that taps pass through. They no longer cover DELVE AGAIN,
  LEAVE SHOP or ROLL. After the guided tour you go straight into your first
  fight, without a card that repeats the lesson. A new player's first delve
  ends without a song-credit pop-up.
- **Reduce Motion** covers the new effects. Foes lean in place instead of
  dashing, and the victory banner fades in without scaling or embers.

Every roll, damage number and block is exactly what the dice said. Your
delvers, kits, dice, saves and Forge unlock stay as they were. Thank you for
keeping the fire lit.

## VERIFIED source and scope

This release packages the six experimental builds (0.184.101–0.184.106) from
[PR #109](https://github.com/tapiwamakandigona/emberdelve/pull/109) at
`7d61e26`. That head already includes #107's 0.184.0 packaging and #108's
living-foes work. Compared with the v0.184.0 source `84f973f`, the non-doc
diff touches presentation (`lib/ui`), tips, the song-credit and tour gates in
`lib/game/controller.dart`, news and the version line. Tests only grow: 11
new test files, and 3 existing ones (song credit, tips, tour) gain cases with
no line removed. Three review harnesses are new. Two existing ones in `tool/`
were corrected to tap dice like a player and to follow the keystone pick, as
disclosed in the loop's progress entries. `lib/sim`,
purchase/entitlement code, `android/` (signing), `.github/` and dependencies
are unchanged. The packaging commit changes only the version, the in-game
news (one 0.185.0 entry replaces the six experimental entries) and docs.
