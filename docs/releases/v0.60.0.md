# v0.60.0 — The Delver's Tally

The delver picker now states your record with each delver.

## What changed

- **The tally.** Every unlocked delver's card on the picker carries a quiet
  line under the name: **"N wins · M delves"** — the same per-delver counters
  the Ledger roster keeps (one vocabulary, two rooms). The moment you pick a
  delver is the moment ownership forms; now the record is there to meet you.
- **Earned, not printed.** A delver you have never delved with shows nothing —
  no "0 delves" line, no clutter on a fresh install. Losses count as delves
  (a record is what happened, not what flattered you), so a delver can
  honestly read "0 wins · 3 delves".
- Locked delvers are unchanged.

## Charter notes

- Zero simulation movement — no seeds, goldens, or balance touched.
- The tally reads live meta counters (cloud-merged like the rest of the
  Ledger); the picker and the Ledger roster can never disagree.
