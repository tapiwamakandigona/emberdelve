# v0.45.0 — The Deeper Song

**One improvement:** the delve now has a second map theme — past the midpoint of every run, the road music darkens into a new piece, **"Deeper Still"** (Kevin MacLeod, "Ossuary 6 - Air", CC BY 4.0), and the track joins the Gramophone as the seventh collectible record.

## What's new

- **A darker song below the midpoint.** The map-family music (map, shop, rest, event, reward, boon) switches from *Into the Delve* to *Deeper Still* once the delver stands at or past the run's halfway layer — the same 0..1 depth signal the Deep Hum ambience has followed since v0.23.0, so the soundscape agrees with itself about how far down you are.
- **A seventh Gramophone record.** *Deeper Still* unlocks the honest way — hear it in play — with the plain hint "Descend past the midpoint of a delve." Once heard, it replays at the hearth like every other record.
- **Combat, boss, victory, defeat and title music are untouched.** Depth darkens only the traveling music.
- **Sourced and processed like its siblings**: same Kevin MacLeod Ossuary family as the title and map themes, trimmed, gained to −19 LUFS (true peak −2.9 dBFS), edge-faded, OGG q4 stereo — full recipe in `PROVENANCE.md`, attribution added to in-app Credits.

## Why this hook

Players told us the music carries the game ("the music is 9/10"). The strongest studios spend that credit on *progression you can hear*: Hades, Slay the Spire and Darkest Dungeon all shift the score as a run deepens, because a mid-run audio turn is felt as stakes rising without a single UI element or number changing. Emberdelve's descent already darkens visually (strata, background grade) and ambiently (Deep Hum) — the melody was the last layer still pretending the fifth floor is the first. Now the second half of every delve *sounds* like the second half. And because the track is also a Gramophone record, first hearing it is a small collectible moment rather than a background change nobody names.

## Deliberately NOT included

- No purchased tracks, no timed unlocks — the record is earned by descending, full stop (spec §Ethics).
- No per-strata micro-switching: one midpoint turn per run keeps the moment meaningful and the crossfades rare.
- No sim changes: depth was already computed gameplay-side (`controller.mapDepth`); this release only listens to it.
