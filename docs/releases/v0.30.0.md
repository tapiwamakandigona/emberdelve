# v0.30.0 — The Delver's Primer (versionCode 56)

Player-facing: **the game finally says what a delve is** — straight from our
first outside review: *"I still don't understand what's a delve but it
doesn't matter I enjoyed the gameplay."* It matters to us.

- The first time the run map comes on screen, one card names the thing you
  are standing in: *"THIS IS A DELVE — one descent into the dark. Pick a
  glowing path, clear each room, and face what waits at the bottom. Win, and
  every ember you gathered comes home. Fall, and the ledger still keeps
  half. Either way the delve ends there — and the next one starts fresh."*
- Shown once, ever — tap anywhere and it never returns. Existing players see
  it once too (the reviewer who asked is one of you); day-one veterans of
  the old tutorial wall are spared.
- The How-to-play deck gains the same card up front, re-readable any time.
- Every claim on the card is literal sim behavior: wins bank every ember,
  deaths keep half (floor 5 + layer reached) — the fair-death ledger from
  v0.3.1, now said out loud.

No sim changes: simVersion 7, all golden anchors byte-identical.
