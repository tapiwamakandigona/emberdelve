# 0.183.0 — Bodies in the Fight

## For players

- Delvers fight with their whole body now. Each signature weapon has its
  own way of being used: a planted sword cut from the shoulder, a maul that
  coils and drops from overhead and lands with a shock, a fang driven
  straight in with a snap back to guard, a chisel that stamps its mark, a
  hook that catches on the pull, a pick that chips. High rolls wind up
  longer and hit harder; low rolls flick. A maximum roll on a small die is
  read as a maximum, not as a fraction of a d12.
- Blocking is a stance. While block is up the delver braces — weight back,
  weapon across the body — and a hit that breaks on the guard sparks instead
  of cutting.
- Wounds show. Landed blows draw blood (delvers) or ichor, embers, soot
  (creatures), some of which stays on the floor for the rest of the fight.
  As health falls, bodies slump, lose colour, breathe hard and, near the
  end, tremble. Enemies carry their damage the same way.
- Weapons now sit in the hand. Every one of the 22 delver sheets has a
  hand socket the grip is pinned to, instead of one shared offset.

None of this changes a number. Every roll, damage value, block and combo is
exactly what the simulation produced before this release. Reduce motion
keeps the informative still cues (guard pose, wounds, stains) and drops the
flying droplets.
