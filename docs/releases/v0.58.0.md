# v0.58.0 — The Remembered Fights

One improvement: **the Ledger's run rows state the worn epithet and the
fights count when the record banked them** (fuller records, v0.57.0) —
identity and effort restated at the moment of memory.

## What changed

- A row whose record carries an `epithet` titles the delver with it:
  "The Kindler, the Unburnt — fell on floor 4 of 9 to Ashglass Sentinel".
  The epithet only appears when the player chose to wear one.
- A row whose record carries `fights` adds one short token to the meta
  line: "hard · 2026-08-25 · 7 fights · tap to copy its Delve Code"
  (singular "1 fight" handled).
- Rows from records that predate the fuller banking render byte-identical
  to before — absent facts stay absent, nothing is invented.
- Restraint check passed at 320 px: the pathological worst case (longest
  delver name + longest epithet + longest killer name) wraps to four
  legible lines; typical rows are unchanged in height.

## Why

v0.57.0 made the Ledger remember more; the card read it back but the
rows didn't. A remembered day should say who you were and what it cost —
the same identity hook The Epithets shipped, now honored in memory.

## Ethics

- Every stated token is a banked fact; old records are untouched.
- Zero sim changes; goldens and pinned sweeps untouched.
