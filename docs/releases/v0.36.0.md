# v0.36.0 — The Epithets

**One improvement:** earned **epithets** — titles worn under your delver's name ("The Kindler, the Unburnt"), unlocked by what you have actually done in the delve. The third limb of customisation: dyes recolor the body (v0.27.0), vistas repaint the world (v0.35.0), epithets wear your history.

## What's new

- **Eight epithets**, chosen from a new THE EPITHET section on the character screen (below dyes and vistas):
  - **the Delver** — *Win a delve.*
  - **the Thorough** — *End ten runs — won, lost, or walked away.*
  - **the Exact** — *End 25 fights on exactly the damage needed.*
  - **the Unresting** — *Win a delve without visiting a single rest site.*
  - **the Bossbane** — *Fell all three bosses of the deep.*
  - **the Emberled** — *Bank 1,000 embers in total.*
  - **the Unburnt** — *Win a delve on Hard.*
  - **the Highborne** — *Climb to ascension 5.*
- The worn title shows under each unlocked delver's name on the character screen, on the run summary, and on the shareable Delver's Card (v0.34.0) — "The Kindler, the Highborne · Hard · Ascension 12" — including the clipboard fallback.
- "No epithet" is always a legal choice. Your pick syncs with cloud saves (fresher side wins); unlocks are derived from your lifetime record, so a title earned anywhere is worn everywhere.

## Deliberately NOT included

- No purchasable titles — every epithet is earned at the fire, never sold (docs/spec.md §Ethics).
- No new counters and no new art — unlocks reuse the Delver's Ledger stat vocabulary already tracked since v0.5.0; zero assets added, install size unchanged.
- No sim changes — simVersion stays 7; runs, seeds, and daily/weekly fairness untouched.
