# v0.87.0 — The Guttering Foe

The most valuable fact in the fight is now visible during it.

The 30% rule spoke three times — the danger music (v0.23.0), the Narrow
Climb (v0.85.0), the Foe's Last Thread (v0.86.0) — but never while the
player could still act on it. The enemy bar read the same red at 90% HP
and at 9%. Now, when the foe is inside the rule, the bar gutters: the fill
turns gold (the player's good-news colour) and the caption reads
ENEMY HP · NEARLY SPENT · TURN N. Spend the surge die or bank the block —
now you decide with the fact on the table.

## Honesty rules

- One shared `inTheRed()` helper now owns the rule; the danger bed and
  both summary lines call it, so the four voices can never disagree.
  (The rule had been hand-copied in three places — drift risk retired.)
- Colour + text, no animation: reduce-motion players get the full tell,
  and the hot combat path stays cheap.
- No "one more hit" math — that depends on assignment, relics, and block,
  and the game would sometimes be wrong. A fact, never a promise.
- Screen readers hear it free: the bar's semantics speak the caption.

## Technical

- `inTheRed(hp, maxHp)` in the controller; enemy panel picks colour and
  caption from it; `_inDanger` and both summary helpers refactored onto
  it, behaviour-identical. Tests +3 (`test/guttering_foe_test.dart`:
  boundary pins + gold/red widget drives). Plate-critiqued at 360x640 and
  320x568 (two-digit turn). Zero assets, zero sim movement.
