# v0.20.0 — The Living Ladder

The Ascension ladder is Emberdelve's endgame: twenty rungs of stacked
challenge, each unlocked only by winning the one below it. This release
is an honesty fix for that ladder.

## What we found

We swept the ladder with our balance bot (100 seeded runs per character
per rung, on HARD): win rates hit **0.0% at rung 12 and above, for every
character** — and losses piled up on the very first floors at every rung.
Since rung N+1 only unlocks by winning rung N, rungs 13–20 were
effectively decorative. We sell "20 rungs" and award a badge for reaching
rung 20; math that makes those promises unkeepable is a bug, not a
difficulty setting.

## What changed

- **The flat ramp is gone.** The old math added the full rung number to
  every enemy hit from floor one — rung 12 meant +12 damage at the door.
  It was a bouncer, not a climb (the same failure our HARD rework fixed
  back in v0.3.3).
- **Rungs now scale by depth**, exactly like HARD's staircase: at rung 20
  the early floors take +3, mid-delve +5, and the deep floors and boss +7.
  The door stays fair; the bite lives where your build should be ready
  for it.
- **Every rung now genuinely counts:** enemies gain +1% HP per rung, so
  stepping from rung 6 to 7 changes the fight even between attack-ramp
  steps.
- **The ladder is provably alive.** After the change, our bot — a *lower*
  bound on human play — wins at rung 20 with every character. Those
  winning seeds are pinned in the test suite: the build fails if a future
  change ever makes the top of the ladder unwinnable again.

Measured after (bot win rate on HARD): rung 1 ≈ 22–45%, rung 8 ≈ 13–27%,
rung 20 ≈ 3–13% depending on character. Steep, as an endgame should be —
but a climb with a top, not a wall with a poster of one.

## What didn't change

- Rung 0, all difficulties: byte-identical to v0.19.0 (our golden anchor
  proves it). Dailies, weeklies, and shared seed challenges are all
  rung-agnostic or rung 0.
- Saves are safe: a fight already in progress keeps its old numbers;
  only new encounters use the new ramp.
- No new content, no price changes, nothing moved in the UI beyond one
  sentence describing the ladder more precisely.

## Under the hood

- `ascensionAttackBonus(rung, layer)` and `ascensionHpScalar(rung)` are
  deterministic pure functions — no RNG touched, replays deterministic.
- New gate `test/ascension_ladder_test.dart`: rung-0 neutrality,
  monotone ramps, and pinned A20 wins for all four characters.
- Sweep + histogram + seed-hunt probes live in `tool/` for the next
  balance pass. Design notes with full before/after tables:
  `docs/improvements/v0.20.0-living-ladder-design.md`.
