# v0.104.0 — The Delve Itself

A player review said it plainly: "I still don't understand what's a
delve but it doesn't matter." It matters. The Codex answered 'who is
this enemy' and 'what is this relic' but never the first question a
new delver actually asks — the world itself had no page.

- The Codex gains a third kind: PLACE. Eight entries of the world's
  own words — The Delve, The Ember, The Hearth, The Depths, The
  Dice, The Forge, The Provings, The Vistas — shelved FIRST, under
  THE WORLD, with 'what is a delve?' at the very top of the book.
- Places are the cheapest lore in the book at 10 embers (common
  enemies 15, elites 20, bosses 30, relics 20): the first question
  should be the cheapest to answer. Same charter as everything in
  the Codex — flavor only, prices up front, nothing mechanical ever
  paywalled.
- The unsealed count, the summary's codex pull, and the Ledger meter
  all include the new shelf automatically (86 entries total).
