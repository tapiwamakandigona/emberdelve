# Emberdelve v0.65.0 — The Charted Depth

**One improvement:** the delver picker now charts each character's deepest
floor beside their tally — `5 wins · 12 delves · floor 6` — read straight
from the ledger, never estimated.

## What changed

- **Per-delver depth records.** `MetaState.charBestFloor` banks the deepest
  1-based layer each character ever stood on, won or lost, in the same
  breath as the profile-wide `bestFloor`.
- **The picker says it.** The v0.60.0 tally line gains `· floor N` when a
  charted depth exists. A delver with runs but no provable depth shows the
  plain tally — no guessed floors, ever.
- **Older saves chart what they can prove.** Pre-v0.65.0 profiles seed
  per-delver depths from the run history (per-character max of recorded
  floors), the same honesty rule bestFloor used in v0.5.0. Saves that
  already carry the key never re-seed.
- **Cloud merge:** per-key MAX, the charRuns/charWins convention — a depth
  earned on any device stands on all of them.

## Why

Per-character records are identity: a player who mains one delver and
dabbles in another has two stories, and the picker told neither. After a
loss, a weaker delver's own record ("the peddler's floor 3") is a goal you
can beat tonight even when the profile record is not — loss-retention
evidence says that local goal is what brings a player back.

## Integrity

- Zero sim movement — pinned seeds and all goldens unchanged.
- Save format: additive key, omitted when empty; pre-charted saves stay
  byte-identical through the transfer codec (fixture extended to prove it).
- 689/689 tests green; analyzer clean with fatal-infos.
