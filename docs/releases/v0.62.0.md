# v0.62.0 — The Kept Fire

Come back after a while and the hearth says so.

## The one improvement

One warm line on the title, shown only when your newest remembered run is
seven or more calendar days old:

> 23 days since your last delve — the hearth kept its fire.

- The true count of days, stated once, under the gathered hearth.
- **Nothing decayed while you were gone.** No streak was counted, no
  bonus expired, nothing is owed. The line exists to say exactly that.
- It retires itself: finish any delve and the hearth goes back to its
  usual quiet.
- Fresh profiles and active players never see it.

## Zero sim movement

No simulation, balance, or save-format change — the line is derived
entirely from the run history the Ledger already keeps. All pinned seeds,
goldens, provings, and delve codes remain valid.
