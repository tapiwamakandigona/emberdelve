# 0.186.0 — Clear Choices

## For players

- **Every call-out has a place.** Call-outs such as STRAIGHT!, FREE REROLL
  NEXT TURN and +5 EMBERS — EXACT! each hold one spot for as long as they
  show. That spot is either above both fighters' heads or in the strip just
  under your HP bar. They never sit on your hero, a foe or your HP, and at
  the standard text size they are never drawn smaller than 12 sp. When there
  is no room, a call-out waits its turn instead of landing on top of another.
  Holding a badge or chip to hear what it means now opens a solid help box.
- **The final blow.** A slain boss no longer holds a flat white block. The
  flash lasts only the moment of impact, the body burns ember-hot, and it
  crumbles from that glowing body. Under Reduce Motion it never flashes
  white; its colour drains to grey as it fades.
- **VICTORY! stands alone.** When the last boss falls, the other call-outs,
  damage numbers and help boxes clear the stage as the banner lands, and
  nothing new is called out over it. The banner sits above the fighters,
  or beside your hero on small phones, and never across your hero or their
  raised weapon.
- **A steadier screen.** A heavy hit shakes the cavern and the fighters.
  The top bar, your HP and the dice stay still.
- **Livelier foes.** Once in each idle loop, brutes and the big bosses roll
  their shoulders and crawlers twitch. Reduce Motion keeps them still.
- **Cleaner hits.** A cut's trail now follows the blade and closes onto its
  edge, and the foe takes a short nick with two sparks instead of a
  crescent. Damage numbers have a hard dark outline, so they read even over
  a white hit flash. Your own damage number moves off your hero wherever the
  stage has room, and a burn chip leaves with the foe it was burning.
- **Clearer choices.** In the guided first fight, the "Roll, then spend"
  card now explains the ATTACK and BLOCK preview, and that END TURN lets the
  foe make the move shown above its head. This is also in French, Spanish
  and Brazilian Portuguese. The Ember Forge offer scrolls instead of
  squeezing on small phones and with large text. It lists what stays free:
  full runs, every delver, and the Daily and Weekly Delves. It also says
  "Pay once. Keep it. No subscription." and has a "Not now — keep playing"
  button. The price is still the one Google Play shows you.
- **A better fit.** The title screen fits a 412x915 phone without
  scrolling again. The game draws edge to edge, and its buttons keep clear
  of the system bars.

Every roll, damage number and block is exactly what the dice said. Your
delvers, kits, dice, saves and Forge unlock stay as they were. Thank you for
delving with us.

## VERIFIED source and scope

This release is the `legacy/dice-builder` lane: v0.185.0 (`c08a0d5`), plus
PRs #112–#125, plus one packaging commit. Every PR head had green CI before
it merged: analyze+test, plus iOS for every code PR (#120 is docs only).
Every code change also had an independent read-only review. The one that
failed review (#121, a test that could not fail) was replaced by #124, which
passed.

Compared with `c08a0d5`, the code diff touches presentation (`lib/ui`,
14 files). It also touches:
- one tutorial card's text in the l10n catalog and its source
  `tool/l10n/interface.json`;
- the edge-to-edge call in `lib/main.dart`;
- the in-game news;
- the version line.

These are unchanged: `lib/sim`, `lib/meta` (including the store and
entitlement code), `lib/game`, `android/` (signing), `ios/`, `.github/`,
`assets/` and the dependencies (`pubspec.lock`).

Tests:
- 12 new test files.
- `test/victory_beat_test.dart` only gains cases.
- `test/readout_lanes_test.dart` moved to the new call-out lane API and got
  stricter; the independent review found no weakened assertion.
- One existing test, `test/marked_week_test.dart`, was corrected with my
  explicit go-ahead (2026-10-03) because it contradicted itself in Short Road
  weeks. It wanted `short_road` both present and absent in the banked
  mutators; the game banks a short week as `short`.

Two review harnesses are new in `tool/`. The packaging commit changes only
the version, the in-game news and docs.
