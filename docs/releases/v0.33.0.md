# v0.33.0 — The Gramophone

The soundtrack our first reviewer scored 9/10 is now a collection you can
keep and replay.

## The Gramophone

- **A new section in The Ledger**: all six tracks of the Emberdelve score,
  kept by the fire. Tap any track you've heard to play it right there; tap
  again to stop. Leaving the Ledger hands the speakers back to the hearth.
- **Tracks unlock by simply playing.** Each locked row says plainly how it's
  earned — begin a delve, meet a foe, stand before a crowned foe, win, or
  lose. Nothing is sold, nothing expires, and a track once heard is yours
  for good.
- Your record syncs with cloud saves (union — two devices' collections
  merge, never shrink).

## Under the hood

- `MetaState.heardTracks` persisted alongside the rest of your record;
  pre-v0.33 saves migrate silently (the hearth theme is seeded on first
  boot — every delver has heard it).
- Track detection is gameplay-owned (computed from the same rule the audio
  layer uses), so it's fully covered by the test suite: 7 new pins including
  catalog integrity, save round-trip, cloud merge, and a full bot-driven
  win/loss pair.
