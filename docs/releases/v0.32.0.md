# v0.32.0 — The Open Rung (versionCode 58)

Player-facing: **an earned Ascension rung finally gets its moment.** For Ember
Forge delvers, a win at your highest rung has always quietly opened the next
one — you had to visit the Ledger to notice. Now the win summary says it,
once, in the moment you earned it:

> *Rung N of the Ascension now stands open.*

- Shown only when THIS win actually raised your best rung — a repeat win at a
  lower rung says nothing, and rung 20 is still the top of the ladder.
- Forge delvers only. A free profile's first win still banks the ledger fact,
  but the summary stays silent — we don't dangle a ladder you can't climb
  (that would be a sales line dressed as praise, and the charter says no).
- No button, no price talk. The character screen's stepper is where climbing
  starts, exactly as before.

Retention hook #7 (mastery ladder surfacing) from
docs/research/retention-hooks-2026-08-23.md; design in
docs/improvements/v0.32.0-open-rung-design.md.

Nothing else changed: sim untouched (simVersion 7), no new persistence, no
new permissions.
