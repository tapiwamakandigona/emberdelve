# Emberdelve v0.13.0 — "The Delver's Rank"

One improvement, GitHub-only release: a rank that names how far you have
already come.

## The Delver's Rank

- The Ledger now opens with your rank — nine named tiers, Ashfoot to
  Deepfire Sovereign, each with a line of its own.
- Rank is computed from what your profile has **already banked**: delves
  won, bosses put down, distinct foes met and felled, codex entries
  unsealed, Daily and Weekly Delves finished. Every mark is real history;
  nothing is estimated and nothing is a teaser.
- The Ledger shows your exact marks and the honest distance to the next
  tier. At the top of the ladder it says so — no invented tier beyond it.
- When a run's banking crosses a tier, the summary adds one quiet line:
  *"You delve as a Sparktender now."* No badge rain.

## The charter, kept

- Your rank never expires, never resets, and is never compared to anyone
  else's.
- Hard and Ascension wins count exactly as "a win" — the rank never leans
  on the paid difficulty.
- Rank is **derived, never stored**: it needs no new save data, and a cloud
  merge can never rank you down (pinned by test).

## Under the hood

- New tier table `data/ranks.dart` (content as data) + pure
  `rankFor(MetaState)` in `meta/rank.dart`; zero sim contact, zero new
  persisted fields, no golden movement.
- Thresholds tuned so a first evening lands 2–3 rank-ups and the top tier
  is a long, honest climb (tuning pinned in `test/rank_test.dart`).

## Why this, now

Backlog `docs/improvements/studio-priorities-2026-08-16.md`, lesson 1: meta
progression is what carries players past day 7–14. The Ledger and bestiary
gave every run visible consequences; the rank gives all of it one name that
only ever climbs.
