# v0.101.0 — The Delver's Page

The Ledger remembers thirty delves across six delvers, in one
undifferentiated pile. "How is my Warden actually doing?" had no
answer short of reading every row.

- When the remembered delves belong to more than one delver, RECENT
  DELVES gains pages: 'All delvers', plus one page per delver who has
  actually delved. Chips wear given names ('Ashka Emberborn', not
  'The Kindler') and appear only for delvers with real records — no
  empty pages, no teaser chips.
- The sounding line redraws from the open page, so a delver's page
  shows THEIR depth arc. Its two-record gate applies to the page: one
  remembered delve stays a row, exactly as at v0.77.0.
- Reading aid only: the open page is ephemeral (never persisted — an
  open page is not progress), records are untouched, and 'All
  delvers' is always one tap away.

## Technical

- LedgerScreen goes stateful with an ephemeral `_delverPage`;
  `_delversRemembered` derives pages from runHistory in roster order;
  `_pageHistory` filters without reordering.
- test/delver_page_test.dart (5): no pages for one delver, page-per-
  remembered-delver only, filter + restore, line-redraw + honest gate,
  ephemerality (toJson carries no page key).
- Plates: all-page and Warden-page at 360x640, wrap restraint at
