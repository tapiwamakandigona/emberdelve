# v0.71.0 — The First Words

One improvement: **a brand-new hearth now tells you what a delve is.** A
player finished easy mode and told us, kindly, that they still didn't know
— the tour teaches *how* (roll, pick, spend) and the subtitle is a genre
tag, but nothing anywhere stated the premise. Now the title screen of a
fresh profile says it plainly, once:

> The delve is the dark below the hearth — floor under floor of it. Go
> down with your dice, come back with the Ember.

## What changed

- **One quiet inscription on the title screen**, shown only while the
  profile has no ended run. The moment your first run banks — win, loss,
  or walk-away — it steps aside forever.
- **Veterans never see it.** No tour re-show, no popup, no new save data —
  pure derived state, the same self-clearing pattern as the kept-fire
  line (and the two can never collide: one needs a remembered run, the
  other needs none).

## Under the hood

- `firstWordsLine(MetaState)` beside `keptFireLine` in the title screen;
  key `first-words`; micro type, wraps cleanly at 320dp.
- Zero sim movement, zero save-format movement. Suite green, analyze
  clean (fatal-infos).

## Ethics

States the fiction, asks nothing. Shown exactly once in a profile's life,
at the only moment it answers a real question.
