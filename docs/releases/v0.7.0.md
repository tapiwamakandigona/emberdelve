# v0.7.0 — "The Face Forge"

## Temper a face
At any rest, mark one face of one die — once per delve.
- **Blade** — that face pays +2 attack
- **Aegis** — that face pays +2 block
- **Surge** — landing it naturally hands back a reroll
- **Echo** — landing it arms +1 for the opposite verb

A tempered die wears its mark in the tray, and the mark lights up when the roll
actually lands on it.

## Choose a keystone
After your first won fight, take one of three (or decline):
- **Ashen Edge** — first attack each turn +1 per other unspent die
- **Living Bastion** — half the block you did NOT need carries over
- **Crown of Twelve** — +1 per extra die size used this turn
- **Twin Bellows** — alternating attack/block pays +1, +2, +3
