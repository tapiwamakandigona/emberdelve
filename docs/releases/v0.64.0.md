# v0.64.0 — The Deepshale

One improvement: a sixth vista, earned by the depth record.

## What changed

- **New vista: Deepshale.** Unlock line: *"Stand on the ninth floor."* Reach
  the ninth layer of the delve — in a run you won *or* lost — and the deep's
  own slate joins your wardrobe.
- It is the first vista that **drains** color instead of raising it: a cold
  grey-slate grade (slight hue shift toward blue, saturation pulled down,
  a faint dark wash) that makes the delve read like bare rock.
- Like every vista, it is earned by delving and never sold.

## Details

- The unlock keys off your existing depth record (`bestFloor`), so veterans
  who have already stood on the ninth floor find Deepshale waiting the moment
  they open the wardrobe.
- Vista picker, unlock lines, and selection behave exactly as before; the new
  card appears last on the shelf.

## No balance changes

No simulation, difficulty, drop, or save-format changes. Existing saves,
seeds, and daily delves are untouched.
