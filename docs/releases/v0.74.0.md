# v0.74.0 — The Full Roster

One improvement: **the ledger's delver roster now shows each delver
whole.** Since the identity arc (charted depth → worn title → worn dye →
given name), a delver is the richest object in the game — but the ledger's
stats page still listed them as a person-icon and a win count. The page
players re-read for pleasure now speaks everything they've built:

- their **sprite, in its worn dye**
- their **given name**, with the **worn title** in gold beneath
- the tally, now ending in **how deep they've charted** — `45 wins ·
  80 delves · floor 12`

## What changed

- Unlocked roster rows carry the delver's whole identity, arranged
  exactly like the picker card: name over gold title, sprite at rest.
- **Depth honesty** (the charted-depth rule): a delver with no charted
  floor shows no floor — never a guessed "floor 0".
- **Locked delvers keep their mystery**: lock icon, roster name, `locked`
  — unchanged, teasing nothing.

## Under the hood

- Pure read-surface work in the ledger's `_delverRow`; no new state, no
  save movement, zero sim movement. Suite green (+2 tests), analyze clean
  (fatal-infos).

## Ethics

Shows only what the player already earned and chose. No new counters, no
progress bars toward locked delvers on a reading page.
