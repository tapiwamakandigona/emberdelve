# v0.57.0 — The Fuller Record

One improvement: **new run records bank what the remembered Delver's Card
had to omit** — fights won, the floor trace, and the worn epithet — so a
card shared from the Ledger days later carries the full story again.

## What changed

- Every run banked from this version on remembers three more facts,
  additively (`fights`, a compact `trace` string, `epithet` when worn).
  Old records — and cloud copies — simply lack the keys; nothing migrates,
  nothing breaks.
- A Delver's Card built from a fuller record restores:
  - the **fights won** figure on the tally line,
  - the **floor trace grid**, terminal cell painted from the record's own
    result (🔥 on a win, 🟥 on a fall),
  - the **worn epithet**, on the name line and inside the epitaph — worn-
    at-the-time is a banked fact now, so it is stated.
- Cards from pre-v0.57.0 records keep the v0.56.0 behaviour exactly:
  honesty by omission, nothing invented to fill a line.
- `RunTrace` gained a tiny compact codec (`toCompact`/`fromCompact`),
  roundtrip-tested; a full 9-floor trace costs 9 bytes per record.

## Why

v0.56.0 let any remembered win or loss become a card, but the record was
thinner than the moment: no grid, no fights, no epithet. The fix wasn't to
guess — it was to remember. From today, the Ledger's memory matches the
card's ambition.

## Ethics

- Every stated field is a banked fact; absent keys degrade by omission.
- The trace stays spoiler-free: how each floor went, never what it held.
- Zero sim changes; goldens and pinned sweeps untouched.
