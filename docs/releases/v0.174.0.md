# v0.174.0 — The Honest Fold

A scroll-comfort pass across every scrolling surface.

- New shared shell: **ScrollComfort** — a soft ember-dark fade at the
  bottom edge while more content waits below, and at the top once you
  have scrolled past content. When everything fits, no fades at all.
- Applied to the roster, Ledger, codex, provings, the Post, credits,
  settings, the shop, the title menu, and the run summary. Combat and
  the map scale by design and are untouched.
- Purely visual: no controller, no physics change, taps pass straight
  through, horizontal chip rows unaffected. Fades animate in 160ms.
- Why: primary actions were already pinned outside every scroll, but
  nothing signaled that more waited past the fold — the Wardrobe sat
  invisible under fourteen delver cards.
