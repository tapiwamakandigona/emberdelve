## The Spoken Badge (first-run teaching, R9 §3)

From layer 5 the Vent Ram and Cinder Urchin bring response-puzzle intents —
**charge** (break it by dealing the threshold this turn) and **counter**
(every non-killing strike costs you). Their badge was icons and numbers; the
words that decode them lived only behind an untaught long-press or a sealed
codex entry. Now the **first time an enemy ever declares** a charge, counter,
stagger or attack+block, the existing explain call-out speaks on its own
("CHARGING 34 — DEAL 9 TO BREAK"), once per kind, then never again on any
device (keys in `tipsSeen`, union-merged in cloud). A call-out, not a card:
it fades unaided; nothing to dismiss, no scrolling, no new screen.

Quiet while a tip card, a tour beat or the how-to-play deck is up — the kind
recurs, so it speaks at its next declaration. Plain attack/block badges are
taught by the tour and never speak on their own. The how-to-play card THE
DARK FIGHTS FAIR gains one sentence: "Hold any badge to hear it in words."

A stagger is only ever declared by breaking a charge (no foe patterns one),
so its words ride the CHARGE BROKEN! call-out the first time.

`lib/sim` untouched. Tests: `test/spoken_badge_test.dart` — director
once-ever/quiet/not-consumed, allSeen contract, JSON + cloud-merge
persistence, and three widget pins (a fresh profile hears the first charge
unasked exactly once; an opening charge speaks after the stage settles;
breaking a charge speaks the stagger once).

## The Still Route (performance)

Dragging the map into its end-of-list stretch repainted the entire route every
frame — the gold/ember HUD, the footer line and the backdrop re-rasterized
sixty times a second on top of the map itself (probe: 80 paints per frame at
360×800). The cause was structural: Android's stretch-overscroll indicator
lives inside the map's `LayoutBuilder`, and every setState there scheduled a
relayout that walked up to the enclosing Column and marked paint clear to the
route boundary. The map body is now its own relayout and repaint boundary;
the same drag costs 16 paints per frame, all of them the map's own pulsing
medallions and drifting embers. Pinned by `test/still_route_test.dart`, which
counts HUD text paints during a sixty-frame drag (57 before, 0 after). The
roster, rest, shop and summary lists were measured the same way and were
already still. Probe kept at `tool/scroll_paint_probe_test.dart`.

## The Spoken Stones (content)

Three events join the deck at its end — 56 rooms now — themed to this
release's teaching line: the delve keeps its word, and every price is written
on the wall before you pay it.

- **The Fair Scale** — weigh your gold (−15 gold, +14 embers), weigh your
  wounds (heal 20%), or leave the pans empty.
- **The Two Marks** — cut your marks (−5 hp, +3 max hp), count the older marks
  (+18 gold), or pass by.
- **The First Lantern** — take the ember (−30 gold, a random relic), warm your
  hands (heal 30%), or leave it burning.

Every option is checked against what the sim does (`test/spoken_stones_test.dart`):
labels pay exactly what they say, an unaffordable option is not a command, and
events never kill. The deck is append-only: ids never reorder, so saved
codex and tale references stay valid.
