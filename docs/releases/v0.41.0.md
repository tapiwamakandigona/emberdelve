# v0.41.0 — The Ninth Proving

**One improvement:** the Provings grow from eight to nine — **The Full Purse**, the Peddler's seat at the table, so every delver in the roster now has a named, exact, proven-winnable delve.

## What's new

- **The Full Purse**: the Peddler on normal floors, seed 2 — lean dice the whole way down, with the Kiln Key paying for the climb. Bot-win proven before it earned a name, and the CI winnability proof now pins it forever alongside the other eight.
- A proving you can't start yet still states its requirement as a quiet fact: "Needs The Peddler at the fire." — never a purchase prompt (the Peddler unlocks with banked embers).
- **Copy fix caught by plate review**: the Provings screen intro hardcoded "Eight named delves… All eight are proven winnable" and went stale the moment a ninth existed. The prose is now count-free; the header's "N of M CLEARED" is the only place a count lives.

## Deliberately NOT included

- No rewards, no rotation, no timers — the Provings charter is unchanged; the clear mark is the whole prize.
- No sim changes — a proving is exact start parameters; simVersion stays 7.
