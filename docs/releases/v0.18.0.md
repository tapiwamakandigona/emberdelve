# v0.18.0 — The Trimmed Wick

**Which file do I download?** `emberdelve-v0.18.0-arm64-v8a.apk`, same as
always — just noticeably smaller this time.

## What's new

- **The download lost about 5 MB.** Nothing was removed from the game —
  no track, no sound, no art, no content. Two under-the-hood trims:
  1. The four music loops (title, map, combat, boss) were re-encoded at a
     leaner Vorbis quality tuned for how game music is actually heard on
     a phone. We checked our work: loop points are **sample-exact** against
     the originals (no skips, no gaps), and the only measurable difference
     is above ~16.5 kHz — at the edge of hearing and above what phone
     speakers reproduce. If you play on studio monitors and disagree,
     open an issue; we kept the masters.
  2. The engine no longer packs debug symbols into the app binary. Those
     symbols now live in a build artifact on our side, so crash reports
     can still be decoded — you just don't have to carry them around.
- **No gameplay changes.** Saves, balance, prices, content: untouched.

## Why

You should not pay storage for bytes that do nothing for you. This is the
second structural size fix (v0.14.0 stopped shipping three CPU families in
one file); it's also a promise about direction — the download shrinks when
we find honest fat, and when it grows, the release notes will say exactly
which content you got for it.

## Under the hood

- Music: Vorbis q2 re-encode, `duration_ts` verified equal per track;
  sfx untouched (they're gated by an offline true-peak headroom check).
- Builds: `--split-debug-info` on APKs and AAB; symbols retained as a CI
  artifact for `flutter symbolize`.
- Sim and save format unchanged; simVersion unchanged.
