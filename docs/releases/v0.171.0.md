# v0.171.0 — The Collier

The fourteenth delver: the charcoal-burner — the first kit whose whole
pool arrives worked.

## The kit

- **27 HP · Coal Rake · d6/d6/d6**, no relic.
- Three tier-1 smith's marks, one per die: a **Blade** on the first six,
  a **Gilt** on the second, a **Mend** banked on the third die's low
  face. Nothing plain: every die out of the same clamp.
- Smith's work, not the player's: temper cap and the Six Marks bank
  untouched (pinned).
- Unlock **1800 embers** (ladder stays ascending), roster index 13.
