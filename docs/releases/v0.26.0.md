# v0.26.0 — The Widened Hearth

**One improvement:** Emberdelve now looks right on tablets.

## What changed

On an 8–10" tablet in portrait, every menu-family screen used to stretch its
buttons and cards edge to edge across the full 800dp — a Delve button the
width of a dinner tray, codex cards like park benches. Now:

- Menu and column screens (title, delver select, boon, keystone, event,
  rest, reward, shop, run summary, settings, ledger, codex, past posts,
  credits) cap their content at a comfortable reading width (560dp) and
  center it. Full-bleed layers — the vignette, drifting embers, the parallax
  cave walls, and the in-run HUD strip — still fill the whole screen, so
  nothing looks letterboxed.
- Combat and the map are untouched. They scale by design and already read
  well at tablet widths.
- The overflow gate now sweeps two tablet-portrait sizes (800×1280 and
  1600×2560) on top of the five phone sizes, at normal and 1.3× text scale,
  so this stays fixed.

Phones are byte-identical: at widths up to 560dp the clamp never engages.
