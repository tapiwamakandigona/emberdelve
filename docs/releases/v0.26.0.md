# v0.26.0 — The Guided Delve (versionCode 52)

Player-facing: the anchored onboarding tour. New players reported not
knowing how to play (WhatsApp feedback, Aug 2026) despite the contextual
tip cards — the cards describe the UI instead of pointing at it.

- Spotlight tour in the first fight, anchored to the LIVE widgets: dice
  tray → ROLL, pick a die, ATTACK/BLOCK, the enemy intent badge, the risky
  reroll. Action steps complete only when the player performs the action;
  info steps are tap-to-continue. Always skippable ("n of 5" + SKIP).
- `MetaState.tourSeenVersion` (cloud merge: max) — every profile below the
  current tour version sees it once, INCLUDING veterans of the old cards.
- Settings → "Replay the guided tour in your next fight."
- Contextual tips stay quiet while the tour is on screen; they still own
  combos/burn/block-fade at their first-contact moments.

Design doc: docs/improvements/onboarding-anchored-tour-2026-08-23.md
Tests: 477 green (12 director/meta unit, 3 combat widget integration).
