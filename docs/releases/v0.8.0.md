# v0.8.0 — Tell the Tale (build 34)

One improvement, shipped on its own (GitHub-only release; no Play submission
per owner directive 2026-08-16): **the share artifact**.

## For players

Your finished delve now leaves a tale worth telling:

- **The floor trace.** Every summary screen shows your run as a row of
  squares — 🟩 a floor survived untouched, 🟨 a floor that drew blood,
  🟥 the floor that claimed you, 🔥 the Ember claimed. It's the exact text
  that copies, so what you share is what you saw.
- **Daily & Weekly Delve results now carry the trace.** Same spoiler-free
  promise as before: your result says how it went, never what the shared
  map holds.
- **Seed challenges for every other run.** Finished a normal or seeded
  delve? "Copy seed challenge" puts the seed, difficulty, your trace, and
  the replay path on your clipboard. Same seed, same delve — the map, the
  offers, the rolls, all of it.

No streaks, no expiry pressure, no taunt copy. The text states facts and
stops — that's the house style and it isn't changing.

## Under the hood

- New pure module `lib/game/run_trace.dart`: a `RunTrace` observer fed by
  the same sim event stream the ledger counters use. The sim itself is
  untouched — zero snapshot changes, all golden hashes unchanged.
- The trace rides the autosave beside the sim snapshot (`run_trace` key,
  same side-channel as `run_labels`), so kill + resume mid-run keeps the
  floors already walked — including a half-open floor's damage state.
- TalkBack reads one combined label ("Floor trace: 8 floors, 3 unharmed…"),
  never a row of emoji.
- `dailyShareText` / `weeklyShareText` without a grid remain byte-identical
  to their v0.7.0 output (tested), so nothing shifts for pre-existing copy.

## Why this, first

Research pass `docs/improvements/studio-priorities-2026-08-16.md`: Wordle's
engineered share loop and Balatro's seed culture are the two cheapest
user-capture mechanisms a premium game can own. Both reduce to the same
design: a spoiler-free, instantly recognizable artifact a proud player can
paste anywhere. This release builds Emberdelve's.
