# Emberdelve v0.55.0 — The Duskquartz

The Provings finally feed something you can wear.

## The Duskquartz

- A fifth vista joins the shelf: **Duskquartz** — quartz veins catch the
  last light, and the delve keeps its own dusk. A violet-gold grade laid
  over every layer, from the first hearth to the boss floor.
- Unlocked by **clearing 3 provings** — the first cosmetic the Provings
  have ever fed. The ten puzzles and the vista shelf finally touch.
- Like every vista, it is a color grade, not new art: the download does
  not grow a byte, and the strata's depth grade still composes on top.
- The unlock is derived from your real cleared provings at read time —
  nothing new is saved, so the milestone can never lie or desync.

## Notes

- Zero gameplay changes — same sim, same seeds, same odds.
- Vistas remain milestone unlocks: earned by delving, never sold, never
  timed (see docs/spec.md §Ethics).

Thank you for playing — and to everyone who cleared a proving just to see
what it was for. Now there's an answer.
