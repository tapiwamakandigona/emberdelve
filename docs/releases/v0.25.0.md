# v0.25.0 — The Unquiet Deep

One improvement, as promised: **content drop 3**. The deep is unsettled
after the fall of the Hearthless King, and the paths between fights have
grown stranger.

## What's new

**Six new events** (deck 33 → 39). The event pick is uniform over rooms you
have not yet seen this run, so a bigger deck directly means an evening of
runs repeats itself less:

- **The Toppled Crown** — a throne of slag on its side, crown still wedged
  beneath it.
- **Soot Choir** — wisps humming in rounds around a dead chimney.
- **The Bridge Keeper** — a rope bridge over a char pit, and a keeper who
  remembers every crossing.
- **Cinder Hermit** — he trades in dice, and he is not lonely, thank you.
- **Glowworm Hollow** — the first thing down here that asks nothing of you.
- **The Pale Lode** — the King's fall shook loose more than thrones.

**Four new relics** (pool 28 → 32), each a combination of two familiar
effects — nothing new to learn, new ways to lean:

- **Drowned Bell** — every combat turn starts with +1 block, rests heal +3.
- **Ashglass Prism** — max rolls pay +2 gold, won fights pay +2 embers.
- **Wyrmscale Cloak** — blocks gain +1, attackers take 2 damage back.
- **Choir Censer** — won fights heal 2 and pay +3 gold.

Each relic has its story in the Codex.

**One honesty fix:** an event outcome that changes nothing (a bathe at full
health, walking away) used to resolve silently. Every choice now states its
concrete outcome — including "Nothing changed".

## Fairness accounting

Content lives as data over the sealed simulation's fixed effect vocabulary —
zero new rules, zero resolution changes. Growing the deck and relic pool
re-rolls what every seeded run draws, so the golden anchors were
deliberately re-anchored (fourth re-anchor; procedure and old → new table in
the repo). Every per-boss anchor seed was re-proven to actually fight its
boss before pinning.

Bot win rates after the drop, against the published fairness bands:

| Difficulty | Band | Measured |
|---|---|---|
| Easy | 80–90% | 89.0% (n=400) |
| Normal | 55–70% | 64.25% (n=400) |
| Hard | 30–45% | 41.3% (n=1000; pre-drop same-probe baseline 43.2% — difference within sampling noise) |

A20 remains provably alive for all four delvers (re-hunted, four distinct
winning seeds pinned).
