# 0.182.0 — Keepers of the Flame

## For players

- A thank-you for everyone who already owns the Ember Forge, and new owners
  too. Add a private nickname and crest to the title, or skip it. Edit, hide
  or remove either in Settings. No extra payment and no gameplay advantage.
- Twenty-two existing delvers get distinct models, not another set of
  recolours. Their IDs, equipment, unlocks and saved progress are unchanged.
- French, Spanish and Brazilian Portuguese for first-session menus, the
  how-to-play, main combat controls, comfort settings and Keeper editor.
  Device language is automatic; Settings has a manual choice. Advanced
  lore and some descriptions remain English.

## Existing owners

Saved Forge ownership qualifies on the next title visit. A confirmed Play
restore also qualifies, including after reinstall. Restore with the Play
account used to buy the Forge. A nickname stays on this device and is not
copied through cloud saves or public credits.

## VERIFIED engineering evidence — September 8

- Before version bump: analyzer clean, 1,348 tests pass; original tests and
  simulation/entitlement/telemetry logic unchanged. Release rerun recorded in
  progress after it runs.
- Character images: decoded RGBA footprint 3,732,480 → 675,840 bytes
  (81.89% smaller); 39,127 PNG bytes. This is asset decode memory, not total
  process RAM or measured phone FPS.
- Seven reachable SFX mix scenarios clear the existing 0 dBTP limit;
  full-attack mix is tight at −0.90 dBTP. No audio was changed by this pass.
