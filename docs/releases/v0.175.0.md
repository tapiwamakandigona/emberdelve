# v0.175.0 — The Stoker

A fifteenth chair at the fire: **the Stoker**, the furnace-feeder.

## The kit

- **Three heavy Ember Dice — all d8s, all plain.** Big coals, thrown
  whole. No relic, no smith's marks: the first pouch in the roster whose
  identity is nothing but the weight of the dice.
- **16 HP.** The thinnest skin at the fire. The heavy pouch swept far
  above band at a normal delver's health (94.00/78.75/57.75 at 24 HP
  over 400 seeds); the tension that prices it honestly is a stoker who
  cannot afford many mistakes. Final sweep 89.00/64.75/44.00
  easy/normal/hard vs kindler 89.75/67.25/41.50 — in band.
- **Fire Iron** — the bar that shoves the big coals home. Long reach:
  stokers work at the mouth of the heat, not in it.
- Unlocks at **1950 embers**, after the Collier.

## The mirror

The flintwright carries four small dice; the stoker carries three big
ones. The roster now holds both ends of the weight question, and the
delve answers each differently.

## Also in this release

  and The Six-Handed now count fifteen. The hearth tales recount
  fifteen chairs.
- News: The Stoker.
