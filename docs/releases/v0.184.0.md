# 0.184.0 — The Whole Company

## For players

- All 22 playable delvers now use the September 18 character designs and
  articulated combat bodies. Shoulders, elbows, wrists and boots move with
  the action; the signature tool stays in the moving hand. The six tool
  families keep their own cut, crush, thrust, stamp, hook and pick motions.
- Health, wounds and guard follow the moment of impact instead of changing
  ahead of the blow. This changes the display, not when the simulation
  calculates the result.
- A saved **Blood effects** switch in Settings hides blood, ichor, floor
  stains and wound marks. Damage, guard and fatigue remain visible.
- Tempered dice now use their real die size when choosing visual heat and
  strike strength. Rolls and damage were already correct and are unchanged.

Your delvers, kits, dice, saves and Forge unlock stay as they were.
Thank you for keeping the whole company by the fire.
