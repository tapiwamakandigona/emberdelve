# v0.107.0 — The Unwritten Feats

The Ledger's 43 achievements were authored before the weekly, the
Codex, the hearth tales, the settled scores, and most of the
bestiary existed. Those systems bank real counters — but no feat
ever read them. Metric achievements are the retention doc's own P0
lever (33.96% vs 20.46% D30 on Trophy's data), so the newest
systems deserved the same recognition the oldest ones had.

Seven new feats (50 total):

- **The Shared Seed** — finish a Weekly Delve (weeklies_played 1)
- **Ten Mondays** — finish 10 Weekly Delves (10)
- **The Open Book** — unseal 10 Codex entries
- **The Deep Library** — unseal 40 Codex entries
- **Fireside Regular** — hear 10 hearth tales at rests
- **The Settled Score** — fell a foe that had felled you twice
- **Twenty Names** — fell 20 different foes

Same honesty contract as the originals: every stat is a banked
counter, progress bars can never show a lie, and earning a feat
grants nothing but the mark.

## Technical

- data/achievements.dart: 5 new stats in the vocabulary
  (weeklies_played, codex_unsealed, tales_heard, foes_settled,
  distinct_felled), 7 defs appended.
- meta/achievements.dart resolver: distinct_felled filtered through
  the enemies catalog (delvers_cleared precedent — junk save keys
  can never inflate it).
- test/unwritten_feats_test.dart (4): legality + catalog-bounded
  targets, resolver reads, junk-proofing, ethics sweep. The
  existing 'every stat is wired' guard caught the fixture gap and
  now populates the new counters.
- No new UI: the Ledger's achievement rows, earned-first ordering,
  and progress bars carry the new defs automatically.
