# Emberdelve v0.17.0 — "The Even Scales"

A balance pass — measured, not guessed.

## What changed

We ran every character through hundreds of scripted delves (same seeds,
same bot, all four characters, all three difficulties) and looked at the
numbers. One stood out: **the Warden was quietly winning 85% of HARD
delves** — harder than anyone else wins NORMAL. If you paid for hard
mode, its promise should hold no matter who you bring.

- **The Warden** now starts with a **Slate Chip** (a block-only d4 that
  never rolls below 2) instead of the Ward Iron, and 32 max HP (was 38).
  Her Iron Scale stays. The old Ward Iron isn't gone — the Slate Chip
  **forges directly into it**, so her classic kit is now an earned
  upgrade a few floors in. Her card finally tells the truth, too: her
  offense really is slower now.
- **The Gambler**: 27 max HP (was 26).
- **The Ascetic**: 26 max HP (was 22). Still the glass cannon — just
  less papier-mâché.
- **The Kindler**: untouched. She is the anchor everything is measured
  against.

## The numbers, honestly

Win rates of the same scripted player over 150 fixed seeds per cell
(a bot, not a human — read the *spread*, not the absolute numbers):

| character | hard, before | hard, after |
|-----------|-------------|-------------|
| The Kindler | 50% | 50% |
| The Warden | 85% | 56% |
| The Gambler | 35% | 37% |
| The Ascetic | 24% | 31% |

The gap between the strongest and weakest character on hard shrank from
61 points to 25, and the order now matches the intent: steady tank,
balanced core, coin-flip gambler, glass cannon.

## The fine print, honestly

- **Your saves are untouched.** A run already in progress keeps exactly
  the dice and HP it has. Only new runs use the new kits.
- No prices, unlock costs, or ember values changed.
- The simulation logic itself is unchanged — this is purely starting-kit
  data. Replays and shared seeds from earlier versions still behave as
  they did on those versions.
