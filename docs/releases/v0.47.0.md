# v0.47.0 — The Answered Blow

**One improvement:** enemies that fight back on their own terms. Two new intent kinds turn the telegraph into a **response puzzle** — a question with a specific right answer instead of "block the number, spend the rest" — plus three new foes built around them. Retention research is blunt about this: enemy variety that demands specific responses is what keeps a dice loop from becoming math homework, and the fix is response puzzles, not stat sponges.

## What's new

- **Charge** — the burst check. The badge shows a hit too big to shrug off *and* the number that stops it: deal that much HP damage with your dice this turn and the wind-up collapses into a harmless stagger, live, mid-turn. Turtle if your roll is bad; break it if you can. The break threshold never scales with difficulty or ascension — the answer stays honest.
- **Counter** — the riposte stance. While it bristles, every non-killing strike costs you the shown amount (your block absorbs first). The flurry that wins every other fight is the wrong answer here: save one big die, or wait the beat out. A killing blow lands *before* the riposte — the exact-kill you've been practicing is the clean answer.
- **Three new foes** (late bands only — the early curve stays gentle): the **Vent Ram** (charge drill, layer 5+), the **Cinder Urchin** (counter drill, layer 5+), and the **Magma Lancer** (elite, layer 6+ — riposte, then charge, then a guarded swing: both puzzles as one exam).
- **The badge states the answer.** New intent chips read plainly (charge: the hit + the break number; counter: the per-strike price), long-press explains them in words, screen readers get the full sentence, and breaking a charge pays off loudly on the stage.
- **Three new Codex entries** keep every-enemy coverage exact (Codex 75 → 78).

## Rules, precisely

- Only HP damage from your dice counts toward a break — damage its shield eats does not, and burn/thorns tick after intents resolve.
- An unbroken charge resolves exactly like an attack of its shown amount; blockable as normal. A stagger resolves as nothing.
- The counter stance is the enemy's whole action: it does not also attack that turn.
- Deterministic throughout — both puzzles are pure functions of state and your commands; no RNG was added to resolution.

## Balance — measured, not vibes

- The autoplay bot learned to read the new badges (break when reachable, one clean strike into a counter), so the seeded win-rate bands still measure what a badge-reading player gets.
- 400-seed sweep per difficulty stays in band; 1000-seed hard sweep within drift. Exact numbers in the technical section below.

## Deliberately NOT included

- No new commands and no simVersion bump: saves stay valid, old intents are untouched.
- No fake tension: the near-miss on a break is real arithmetic, not a rigged reveal.
- No early-game gating puzzles — all three foes live where losing to a new mechanic is a lesson, not a bouncer.
