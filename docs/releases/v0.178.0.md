## The Seven Hearths (retention)

Seven hearths wait on the title. Each **distinct local played day**
lights one — whenever those days come. No streaks, no expiry, no
pressure copy: gaps cost nothing and lit hearths keep. The seventh
settles **60 spendable embers** into the pouch (half a delver unlock;
deliberately *not* into `lifetimeEmbers`, which stays a banked-embers
record), and the row retires after settlement day.

Mechanism (written before building, in `progress.md`): the day-7
failure is the absence of a week-scale hook — the morrow trial reaches
day 2 only, the ember ladder is endless. Falsifiable prediction: day-7
retention among new devices moves off 1 within 28 days post-ship, or
the next lever is the opening run.

Cloud merge takes MAX days lit and ORs the settlement flag; a merge or
replay can never pay twice.
