# v0.131.0 — The Written Rules

The Codex gains a sixth kind: **rules**. Six pages on the weekly
calendar — the five singles and the Cold Quarter — in the delve's
own words, sitting between the relics and the dice.

- Priced at 10 embers like places: the calendar is world, not
  tool. Codex grows 98 → 104 entries.
- Names are asserted against the live mutator catalog — a renamed
  rule can never drift from its codex page (the junk-proofing
  habit, applied to prose).
- Closes tonight's rotation arc: play the rules (v0.111), collect
  them (v0.127), wear them (v0.129), now read them.
