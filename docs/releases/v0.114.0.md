# v0.114.0 — The Cold Tales

Three new rooms in the event deck — the winter the doubled week put
into the stone, findable in any run:

- **The Frozen Stall** — a peddler's stall sealed under ice, wares
  still priced. Break it, thaw it, or leave it sealed.
- **The Wintered Die** — a die held mid-roll in clear ice. Crack it
  free (it costs you), sell the curiosity, or let it winter.
- **The Meltwater Pool** — water from far above. Drink deep, or fish
  the coins out of the cold.

53 events; the uniform no-repeat pick means an evening of runs shows
new rooms even later into the night. Every option's label states its
exact price and payout (counted heal labels apply, v0.92.0), every
event keeps an always-legal option (soft-lock guard), and the
fair-death pillar holds: events never kill.
