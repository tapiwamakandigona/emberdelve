# v0.99.0 — The Colored Card

The Delver's Card became SOMEONE'S run step by step — their sprite
(v0.70.0), their dye, their given name, their epitaph. One identity
signal never made it onto the card: the vista. A player who delves
under the Moonveil shares a card in default colors.

- The card background now wears the player's selected vista — the
  exact translucent wash the delve itself wears
  (`Art.backgroundWash` at depth 0), under every fact line.
- Emberlight is the identity: alpha-zero wash, so a player who never
  chose a vista shares a byte-identical card to v0.98.0.
- Portraiture, not history — like the dye, vistas were never banked
  per run, so a remembered card from the Ledger wears the CURRENT
  vista, stated nowhere as historical fact.
- No new words on the card: the vista speaks in color only, so the
  ethics copy sweep is untouched.

## Technical

- `DelverCardFacts.vistaId` (default `defaultVista`); fromController
  reads meta.selectedVista, fromRecord takes `meta?.selectedVista`.
- Card body restructured: clipped Stack with a keyed
  ColoredBox('card-vista-wash') fill under the padded fact column.
- test/colored_card_test.dart: identity default + alpha-0 pin,
  controller/record plumbing, rendered wash color equality.
- Plates: 3x export in all four wash families (identity control,
  cool, gilded, desaturated loss) side by side —
  tool/colored_card_visual_test.dart.
