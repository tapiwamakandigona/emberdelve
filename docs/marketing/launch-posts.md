## 2) r/roguelites — self-promo rules: check "Promotion" flair + participation requirement

**Title:** I built a dice-pool roguelite around one rule: every death is fair (seeded runs, telegraphed enemies, no hidden modifiers)

The design problem I set myself: can a dice game feel high-stakes without ever cheating you? My answer: enemies telegraph every move, dice resolve by fully learnable rules, and runs are seeded end-to-end — replay the seed, make the same choices, get the same result. If you die, the receipt shows it was you.

Run structure: draft/forge a dice pool, combos (pairs, triples, straights) turn junk d4s into your best dice; once per delve you temper a face of one die; after your first won fight you pick a Keystone (or decline) that shapes the run; one risky reroll per turn; exact kills pay bonus embers, overkill splashes. Branching map shows what an elite guards before you commit.

Free full game on Android, offline, no ads — one optional one-time hard-mode unlock. Would love roguelite-nerd feedback on the Keystone balance in particular.
