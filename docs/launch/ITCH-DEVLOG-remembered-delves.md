**Title:** The delve remembers now — six updates on memory

**Body:**

Since the last devlog Emberdelve has shipped six updates, and looking
back they were all secretly about one thing: what the game remembers
about your runs, and what it will admit to.

**The Epitaph (0.54)** — the Delver's Card now carries the run's story
in one or two sentences. Who fell, who ended it, on which floor. Losses
get dignity, not a retry button.

**The Duskquartz (0.55)** — a fifth vista, unlocked by clearing three
provings. Palette only; the delve itself never grows a byte for a
cosmetic.

**Card from the Ledger (0.56)** — the card no longer dies with the
summary screen. Every remembered win or loss in the Ledger (it keeps
your last 30) can become a card, days later. The catch: old records
never banked some facts, so the card omits what it doesn't know rather
than invent it. No zeros pretending to be facts.

**The Fuller Record (0.57)** — so we fixed it at the source. New runs
bank their fights, their floor trace, and the epithet you wore. Nine
floors of trace costs nine bytes. Old records stay exactly as honest as
they were.

**The Remembered Fights (0.58)** — the Ledger rows themselves now say
who you were that day: "The Kindler, the Unburnt — fell on floor 4 of 9
to Ashglass Sentinel · 7 fights."

**The Proven (0.59)** — and the Provings finally crown their victor: a
tenth epithet for clearing all ten. Earned by delving, never sold, like
everything else in this game.

The newest build is on this page. If you've been playing: the next time
you lose a run you're proud of, open the Ledger and share the card. That
moment is what this whole arc was for.

— Tapiwa, Tsoro Studios
