**Title:** Zero to Google Play in 18 days, from Kwekwe, Zimbabwe

**Body:**

Three and a half weeks ago Emberdelve didn't exist. Today it's in public early
access on Google Play, and the newest build (0.23.0) is right here on itch.

I'm a solo dev in Kwekwe, Zimbabwe. That matters for one practical reason: most
payment processors don't work here, so this game runs on the most old-fashioned
model there is — it's free, and if you like it there's one optional one-time
unlock. No ads, no tracking, no energy timers. If the internet dies, the game
doesn't care. It's fully offline.

What shipped this weekend alone:
- ambient audio that hums louder the deeper you descend
- a boss for the lower floors
- a watchtower system that changes how you plan routes
- more balance passes than I want to admit

Honest disclosure, because you deserve it: I use AI tools heavily for code and
text. The design decisions, the balance mistakes, and the 2am fixes are mine.

If you try it, the feedback I need most is simple: what confused you in the
first ten minutes? That's where the next update comes from.

— Tapiwa (Tsoro Studios)
