# R3 supplement — why trust-as-headline works, and its limits
2026-09-01 · feeds the Play relaunch copy (listing FROZEN; doc only)
Sources: web research this date — tann.itch.io/sd-android-only, Slice
& Dice Play listing, Balancy no-ads offer teardown, Dream Games/Royal
Match "no ads" brand analyses (LinkedIn, 2026), ChessElementa
publishing postmortem (2026-02), JMS monetization-preference study
(Indonesia, 2026), BADLAND GDC postmortem.

## The mechanism, in one paragraph
Trust-as-headline works because the premium buyer's real question is
not "is this fun?" (screenshots answer that) but "how will this game
mistreat me?" A decade of ad-gate/timer/gacha games trained mobile
players to scan for the trap before the hook. A headline that answers
the trap-question FIRST ("no ads / no mtx / offline / everything
visible") removes the objection at the exact moment it forms — it is
objection-handling placed before the pitch. [web, 2026-09-01]

## Evidence collected
- **Slice & Dice** sells with a trust LIST as the literal product
  description: "Offline, low battery, no ads/ai/mtx/data, turn-based
  with undo." The Play listing leads features with "No hidden
  mechanics, everything is visible at all times" and frames undo as
  "each turn is like a mini-puzzle." Fun claims come AFTER trust
  claims. [listing, 2026-09-01]
- **Even F2P giants borrowed it**: Dream Games built Royal Match's
  brand on "no ads" in UA creatives; analysts note it "helped with
  trust, store conversion." The 2026 reversal (ads added once the
  math changed) shows players treat the promise as identity — the
  backlash is the proof of how much the promise was worth.
  [web, 2026-09-01]
- **The market is a niche, not a mass**: a 2026 monetization-
  preference study puts premium-no-ads preference at ~15% of mobile
  players (free-with-ads 54%). Trust copy converts the RIGHT 15%
  harder; it does not enlarge the pool. [web, 2026-09-01]
- **Conversion is not traffic**: ChessElementa's postmortem —
  Play "doesn't push new releases… weighs historical download volume
  heavily. Newly listed cold-start apps get essentially zero
  traffic." Matches our reality (38 lifetime installs). A perfect
  listing multiplies visitors it never gets. [web, 2026-09-01]

## What this means for Emberdelve's relaunch copy
1. R3's short-desc proposal already follows the mechanism (trust
   clause "no ads, plays offline" inside 74 chars) — keep.
2. Long description on apply day: move the trust block to the FIRST
   line, not merely "lifted under the opening line." Slice & Dice
   shows the trust list can BE the opening. Candidate first line:
   "No ads, no timers, no gacha. Offline. One $4.99 unlock — the
   free game is complete." (All claims verified true today.)
3. "Everything visible" is our claim too (intents telegraphed, no
   hidden math) — say it in the features list in those words; it is
   the genre-native trust phrase buyers already recognize.
4. Traffic, not conversion, is the binding constraint at 38 installs.
   The only owner-approved external channel today is the itch devlog
   (tsorostudios.itch.io/emberdelve). When shipping unfreezes, the
   cheapest traffic experiment is a free itch demo build linking to
   Play — Slice & Dice's demo-first funnel is the template. Parked:
   product/channel decision, owner's call.

Nothing here changes the frozen listing; it sharpens what gets pasted
on unfreeze day.
