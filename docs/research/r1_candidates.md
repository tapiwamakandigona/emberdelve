## Second-pass notes [web, 2026-09-01]

- **Slice & Dice**: free demo = first 12 levels (was 20); full 20-level
  run, lose-one-fight-restart. Play copy leads with "undo as much as
  you like, no hidden mechanics, everything visible" — trust/clarity as
  the sell. Android demo APK exists on itch (66 MB — 3.2x our full app).
- **Dicey Dungeons**: first launch drops you IMMEDIATELY into Warrior
  ep.1 ("The Warrior's Welcome") — no menu detour; ep.1 speedruns ~4
  min, so a normal first run ≈ 15-25 min, 6 floors.
- **Dice of Kalma**: reviews stress "minimalistic, pared back, core
  loop first" — pacing benchmark: "rapid dice slots with death".
- Genre retention meta: daily missions > daily login rewards (Mistplay
  2026); our daily/morrow/weekly + Seven Hearths already match the
  no-login-reward, play-based pattern — the honest version of this.
