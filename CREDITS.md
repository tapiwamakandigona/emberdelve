# Emberdelve — Credits & Licenses

Emberdelve is made by Tsoro Studios. As of the 2026-07-25 original-asset
pass, **no shipped asset legally requires attribution**: everything below is
either original work by Tsoro Studios or third-party work released under
CC0 1.0 (public-domain dedication, no attribution required). The credits on
this page are kept as a **courtesy** — thank you to these artists for
releasing their work freely.

## Original work (Tsoro Studios)

Created for Emberdelve, generated by scripts in `tool/` (no third-party
samples, images, or soundfonts used):

- **Music** — "Delve Below" (title), "Wayfarer's Ledger" (map), "Sparks in
  the Undergrowth" (combat), "Grove Golem's Wrath" (boss), "Embers Fade"
  (defeat theme + sting): original compositions, synthesized in-repo and
  re-mastered for phone speakers in the 2026-07-25 audio pass.
- **Treasure chest** animation, **campfire checkpoint**, **app icon**
  ("ember in the delve"): original pixel art.
